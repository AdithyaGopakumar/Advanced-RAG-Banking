# Pradhan Mantri Jan Dhan Yojana (PMJDY) Account

## Overview
The Pradhan Mantri Jan Dhan Yojana (PMJDY) account is a National Mission for Financial Inclusion initiated by the Government of India. It provides universal access to banking facilities with a zero-balance account, accompanied by in-built insurance and overdraft benefits.

---

## Features and Benefits

### Key features

- Zero Minimum Average Balance (MAB) requirement
- Free RuPay Debit Card
- In-built accident insurance cover
- Access to an overdraft (OD) facility after satisfactory account operation
- Eligible for Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT)

### Benefits

- **Financial Security**: Gain access to formal credit and insurance products.
- **Government Subsidies**: Seamlessly receive LPG subsidies, pension, and other DBT schemes directly into the account.
- **Emergency Funds**: Utilize the overdraft facility for short-term financial needs.

---

## Eligibility

| Criterion | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Age | 10 years and above |
| Citizenship | Indian Citizen |
| KYC | Valid OVDs or simplified KYC (Small Account rules) |
| Existing Accounts | Can be opened by unbanked individuals |

---

## Required Documents
Customers without standard OVDs can open a PMJDY account as a "Small Account" with just a self-attested photograph and signature in the presence of a bank official. See [KYC Documents](../forms/kyc-documents.md) for specifics.

---

## Interest Rates
Balances in the PMJDY account earn standard savings interest rates. For current rates, see [Deposit Interest Rates](../interest-rates/deposit-interest-rates.md).

---

## RuPay Debit Card
A free RuPay ATM-cum-Debit Card is provided to all PMJDY account holders, allowing them to withdraw funds at ATMs and make merchant payments.

---

## Insurance Cover
The RuPay card comes with an in-built accident insurance cover. For accounts opened after August 28, 2018, the accident insurance cover is ₹2 lakh (previously ₹1 lakh). *Note: The card must be used at least once in 90 days to keep the insurance active.*

---

## Overdraft Facility
After 6 months of satisfactory account operation, an overdraft (OD) facility up to ₹10,000 is available. This is restricted to one account per household, preferably the lady of the household.

---

## Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT)
PMJDY accounts are the preferred vehicle for receiving subsidies under various government schemes (like PM-KISAN, LPG subsidy) directly into the beneficiary's account.

---

## How to Apply

### Apply at a branch
Visit any bank branch or authorized Bank Mitra (Business Correspondent) outlet to open a PMJDY account. The onboarding process is designed to be simple and accessible.

---

## Important Information
- A life insurance cover of ₹30,000 is available for eligible accounts opened between August 15, 2014, and January 31, 2015.
- If opened as a "Small Account", strict transaction limits apply (e.g., max ₹1 lakh credit per year) until full KYC is completed.
