# NRO Account

## Overview
The Non-Resident Ordinary (NRO) Account is a Rupee-denominated savings account designed for Non-Resident Indians (NRIs) and Persons of Indian Origin (PIOs) to manage their income earned in India, such as rent, pension, dividends, or interest.

---

## Features and Benefits

### Key features

- Rupee-denominated account maintained in India
- Acceptable deposits include Indian earnings and foreign remittances
- Free International Debit Card
- Joint holding allowed with another NRI or a Resident Indian (on Former or Survivor basis)
- Easily used to pay local Indian expenses (utility bills, EMI, etc.)

### Benefits

- **Income Management**: The perfect vehicle to legally park and manage income generated within India.
- **Convenience**: Execute local payments easily without converting foreign currency.
- **Joint Operations**: Can be held jointly with resident relatives to help manage the account locally.

---

## Eligibility

| Criterion | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Residency Status | Must be a Non-Resident Indian (NRI) or Person of Indian Origin (PIO) / Overseas Citizen of India (OCI) |
| Documentation | Valid passport, proof of NRI status (visa/residence permit), and overseas OVD (Officially Valid Document) |
| Joint Holding | Permitted with other NRIs/PIOs. Permitted with resident Indians on a "Former or Survivor" basis. |

## Repatriation Rules
Balances in an NRO account are **not freely repatriable**. However, NRIs can repatriate up to USD 1 Million per financial year (April-March) for bona fide purposes, subject to the submission of required documentation, including a certificate from a Chartered Accountant (Form 15CB) and an undertaking (Form 15CA) ensuring taxes have been paid.

---

## Tax Implications
Interest earned on an NRO account is subject to Tax Deducted at Source (TDS) at the rate of 30% (plus applicable surcharge and cess). However, NRIs can avail of a lower TDS rate if India has a Double Taxation Avoidance Agreement (DTAA) with their country of residence, provided they submit the required DTAA documents (TRC, Form 10F).

---

## How to Apply
NRIs can apply online through the Bank's website. The application form and KYC documents must be printed, signed, and attested by the Indian Embassy/Notary/Banker overseas, and couriered to the Bank in India.

---

## Account Redesignation on Return to India
When an NRI returns to India for permanent settlement (losing NRI status), the NRO account must be redesignated as a regular Resident Savings Account. The customer must notify the bank immediately upon this change in status.

---

## Important Information
A resident Indian's existing Savings Account can be converted into an NRO account upon their departure from India for employment, studies, or business abroad (i.e., upon gaining NRI status).
