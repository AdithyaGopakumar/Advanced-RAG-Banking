# FCNR Account

## Overview
The Foreign Currency Non-Resident (Bank) or FCNR(B) Account is a term deposit account that allows Non-Resident Indians (NRIs) to hold their savings in freely convertible foreign currencies. This protects the deposit from currency exchange rate fluctuations.

---

## Features and Benefits

### Key features

- Term deposit account (not a savings account)
- Maintained in foreign currencies (e.g., USD, GBP, EUR, JPY, CAD, AUD)
- Principal and interest are fully repatriable
- Tenure ranges from 1 year to 5 years
- Joint holding permitted with other NRIs

### Benefits

- **Exchange Rate Protection**: Avoid foreign exchange risk by keeping funds in the currency of your choice.
- **Tax Exempt**: Interest earned is entirely exempt from Indian Income Tax.
- **Loan Facility**: Avail Rupee loans or foreign currency loans against your FCNR deposits in India.

---

## Eligibility

| Criterion | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Residency Status | Must be a Non-Resident Indian (NRI) or Person of Indian Origin (PIO) / Overseas Citizen of India (OCI) |
| Permitted Currencies | USD, GBP, EUR, JPY, CAD, AUD (subject to Bank offering) |
| Tenure | Minimum 1 year; Maximum 5 years |
| Joint Holding | Permitted with other NRIs/PIOs. Permitted with resident close relative strictly on a "Former or Survivor" basis. |

---

## Required Documents
Standard NRI KYC documents (Passport, Visa/Residence permit, overseas address proof) are required. See [Account Opening Documents](../forms/account-opening-documents.md).

---

## Interest Rates
Interest rates vary by currency and tenure, and are strictly regulated by RBI guidelines. Interest is compounded half-yearly. See [Deposit Interest Rates](../interest-rates/deposit-interest-rates.md).

---

## Fees and Charges
FCNR accounts do not have maintenance fees. However, premature withdrawal penalties may apply. No interest is payable if the deposit is withdrawn before completion of 1 year. See [Account Charges](../charges/account-charges.md).

---

## Repatriation Rules
The entire deposit amount (principal and interest) is freely and fully repatriable outside India without any RBI approval.

---

## Tax Implications
Interest earned on FCNR(B) deposits is completely exempt from Income Tax in India under Section 10(15)(iv)(fa) of the Income Tax Act. Wealth tax is also not applicable.

---

## Maturity and Renewal
The deposit can be automatically renewed upon maturity, principal only or principal plus interest, based on the mandate given at the time of account opening.

---

## How to Apply
NRIs can apply by remitting funds from abroad in permitted currencies or by transferring funds from an existing NRE/FCNR account. The application form and KYC documents must be attested and couriered to the Bank.

---

## Account Redesignation on Return to India
Upon return to India for permanent settlement, the FCNR deposit can be held until maturity. On maturity, it must be converted into a Resident Foreign Currency (RFC) account or a regular Rupee savings account.

---

## Important Information
Funds in an NRO account cannot be used to open an FCNR deposit. FCNR is strictly a term deposit; checking/savings facilities are not available.
