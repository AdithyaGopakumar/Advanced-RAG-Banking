# Salary Account

## Overview
The Salary Account is a specialised zero-balance savings account offered to employees of our corporate partners. It provides premium banking benefits, enhanced digital access, and preferential pricing on loans and credit cards.

---

## Features and Benefits

### Key features

- Zero Minimum Average Balance (MAB) requirement
- Free premium Debit Card with higher daily withdrawal and POS limits
- Preferential interest rates on Personal and Home Loans
- Pre-approved Credit Card offers based on salary credits
- Complimentary unlimited transactions at all Bank ATMs

### Benefits

- **Cost Savings**: Enjoy banking without the stress of maintaining a minimum balance or paying maintenance fees.
- **Convenience**: Access your salary instantly via our mobile app, UPI, or internet banking.
- **Financial Growth**: Seamlessly sweep excess funds into higher-yielding Fixed Deposits automatically.

---

## Eligibility

| Criterion | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Employment | Must be a salaried employee |
| Employer Tie-Up | The employer must have an active Salary Disbursal relationship with the Bank |
| Documentation | Standard KYC proofs plus employment verification |

---

## Required Documents
To open a Salary Account, you need standard KYC documents (Aadhaar, PAN) along with proof of employment (e.g., ID card, employer letter). See our [Account Opening Documents](../forms/account-opening-documents.md) for full details.

---

## Interest Rates
Salary Accounts earn interest at the same rate as regular Savings Accounts, calculated on the daily closing balance. For current rates, see [Deposit Interest Rates](../interest-rates/deposit-interest-rates.md).

---

## Fees and Charges
While the Salary Account is a zero-balance account, certain specialized services (like international debit card usage) may attract charges. For the complete fee structure, refer to [Account Charges](../charges/account-charges.md).

---

## How to Apply
Employees can open a Salary Account through the dedicated portal provided by their HR department, or by visiting a branch with their corporate ID and KYC documents. Our banking representatives also conduct regular onboarding camps at partner corporate offices.

*Note: If salary is not credited to the account for three consecutive months, the account will be automatically converted to a regular Savings Account and standard minimum balance rules will apply.*
