# Minor Account

## Overview
The Minor Account is designed to help parents and guardians build savings for their children's future while gradually introducing them to banking and financial responsibility in a safe, controlled environment.

---

## Features and Benefits

### Key features

- Zero Minimum Average Balance (MAB) requirement
- Customized Photo Debit Card issued in the minor's name (for minors above 10 years)
- Free SMS alerts sent to the registered mobile number of the parent/guardian
- Standing instructions for recurring deposits (RD) to build a corpus
- Restricted daily transaction limits for enhanced security

### Benefits

- **Future Planning**: Build a dedicated corpus for the child's higher education or other milestones.
- **Financial Literacy**: Minors learn to manage money under parental supervision.
- **Security**: Guardian oversight ensures funds are secure and monitored.

---

## Eligibility

| Criterion | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Age | Below 18 years |
| Guardian | Must be operated by a natural guardian (parents) or court-appointed legal guardian |
| Self-Operation | Minors above 10 years can operate the account independently subject to strict balance and transaction limits |
| Documentation | Birth certificate of minor + KYC of guardian |

---

## Required Documents
To open a Minor Account, you must submit the minor's birth certificate (as proof of age) and the complete KYC documents (Aadhaar, PAN) of the guardian. See [Account Opening Documents](../forms/account-opening-documents.md) for specifics.

---

## Interest Rates
Balances in the Minor Account earn the same interest rate as a standard Savings Account. See [Deposit Interest Rates](../interest-rates/deposit-interest-rates.md).

---

## Fees and Charges
The Minor Account is free from minimum balance maintenance charges. Standard charges apply for services beyond the free limits. See [Account Charges](../charges/account-charges.md).

---

## Account Operation Rules
- **Below 10 Years**: The account must be operated solely by the natural or legal guardian.
- **10 to 18 Years**: The minor can be allowed to operate the account independently. However, strict limits apply (e.g., maximum balance of ₹1,00,000; daily withdrawal limit of ₹2,000).
- Overdrafts are strictly prohibited in minor accounts.

---

## Conversion at Age 18
Upon attaining majority (turning 18 years old), the account will be frozen for further debits. The erstwhile minor must submit their own KYC documents and sign a fresh account opening form to convert the account into a regular Savings Account.

---

## How to Apply

### Apply at a branch
The guardian must visit a branch with the minor's birth certificate and the guardian's original KYC documents to open the account.

---

## Important Information
As per the Indian Contract Act, a minor cannot enter into a valid contract. Therefore, the guardian acts on behalf of the minor. The guardian must ensure that the funds are used for the benefit of the minor.
