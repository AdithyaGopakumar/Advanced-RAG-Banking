# Joint Account

## Overview
A Joint Account allows two or more individuals to pool their resources and manage their finances together through shared access. It is ideal for couples, family members, or business partners who need to manage funds collaboratively.

---

## Features and Benefits

### Key features

- Up to 4 joint account holders permitted
- Multiple operation mandates available (E/S, F/S, Jointly, A/S)
- Debit cards can be issued to all authorized operating mandate holders
- Shared cheque book facility
- Consolidated account statement

### Benefits

- **Shared Management**: Easily manage household or shared expenses from a single pool of funds.
- **Convenience**: Either account holder can perform transactions independently (depending on the mandate).
- **Smooth Succession**: Survivorship mandates ensure seamless access to funds in the event of a holder's death.

---

## Operation Modes
Joint accounts can be opened with the following operation mandates:
1. **Either or Survivor (E/S)**: Any one of the two holders can operate the account.
2. **Former or Survivor (F/S)**: Only the first holder can operate the account while alive.
3. **Jointly**: All transactions require signatures from all account holders.
4. **Anyone or Survivor (A/S)**: Any one of the holders (if more than two) can operate the account.

---

## Eligibility

| Criterion | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Number of Holders | Maximum 4 individuals |
| Age | All holders must be 18 years or above (Minors cannot be joint holders) |
| Residency | All holders must share the same residency status (Resident / NRI) |
| KYC | Full KYC required for all joint holders |

---

## Required Documents
All joint holders must submit their individual KYC documents (Aadhaar, PAN). All holders must sign the account opening form and agree to the operation mandate. See [Account Opening Documents](../forms/account-opening-documents.md).

---

## Nomination Rules
Nomination can be made jointly by all account holders. In the event of the death of one holder, the survivor(s) will hold the funds. The nominee's rights only trigger upon the death of *all* joint holders. See [Nomination](../services/nomination.md) for details.

---

## Survivorship and Deceased Holder
If an account has a survivorship mandate (E/S, F/S, A/S), the death of one holder does not freeze the account. The survivor(s) can continue to operate the account or claim the balance by submitting the death certificate of the deceased holder.

---

## How to Apply

### Apply at a branch
All prospective joint holders must visit the branch together to sign the account opening form and mandate instructions.

### Convert existing account
A single account can be converted into a joint account (or vice versa) by submitting a signed request at the home branch, provided the new holder submits their complete KYC.

---

## Important Information
Any change in the operation mandate, addition/deletion of a joint holder, or account closure requires a written request signed by *all* existing joint holders.
