# Basic Savings Bank Deposit Account (BSBDA)

## Overview
The Basic Savings Bank Deposit Account (BSBDA) is an RBI-mandated financial inclusion product. It is a zero-balance account designed to provide basic banking services to all segments of society, ensuring that everyone has access to a secure savings vehicle without the burden of minimum balance requirements.

---

## Features and Benefits

### Key features

- Zero Minimum Average Balance (MAB) requirement
- Free RuPay ATM-cum-Debit Card
- Unlimited free cash deposits at bank branches and ATMs
- Minimum 4 free cash withdrawals per month (including ATM and branch)
- Free passbook facility

### Benefits

- **Inclusive Banking**: Open to all resident individuals regardless of income level.
- **Cost-Free Maintenance**: No charges for non-maintenance of minimum balance or account activation.
- **Convenient Access**: Access funds easily through the RuPay debit card and branch network.

---

## Eligibility

| Criterion | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Age | 18 years and above (Minors can apply jointly or via guardian) |
| Residency | Resident Indian |
| KYC | Basic Officially Valid Documents (OVDs) or simplified KYC |
| Existing Accounts | The applicant cannot hold any other regular Savings Account in the same bank. |

---

## Required Documents
Customers can open a BSBDA with standard OVDs. If standard OVDs are unavailable, a "Small Account" variant can be opened using simplified KYC (self-attested photograph and signature). See [KYC Documents](../forms/kyc-documents.md) for full details.

---

## Interest Rates
BSBDA balances earn the same interest rate as regular Savings Accounts. For current rates, see [Deposit Interest Rates](../interest-rates/deposit-interest-rates.md).

---

## Fees and Charges
The account has no minimum balance maintenance charges. Basic services (deposit, withdrawal up to limits, ATM card) are free. For charges applicable beyond the free limits, see [Account Charges](../charges/account-charges.md).

---

## Transaction Limits
If the account is opened as a "Small Account" (without complete KYC), the following RBI-mandated limits apply:
- Maximum balance at any time: ₹50,000
- Total credits in a financial year: ₹1,00,000
- Total withdrawals/transfers in a month: ₹10,000

## How to Apply

### Apply at a branch
Visit any branch with your KYC documents. The account is primarily designed for physical onboarding to assist customers requiring financial inclusion services.

---

## Important Information
**Account Consolidation**: As per RBI rules, a customer can have only one BSBDA in a bank. If you already have a regular Savings Account in our bank, you must close it within 30 days of opening a BSBDA. If not closed by you, the bank will automatically close the regular account.
