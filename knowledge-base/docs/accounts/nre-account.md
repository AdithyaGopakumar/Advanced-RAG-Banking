# NRE Account

## Overview
The Non-Resident External (NRE) Account is a Rupee-denominated savings account that allows Non-Resident Indians (NRIs) and Persons of Indian Origin (PIOs) to securely park their foreign earnings in India. The funds are fully repatriable, and the interest earned is completely tax-free in India.

---

## Features and Benefits

### Key features

- Rupee-denominated account maintained in India
- Principal and interest amounts are fully repatriable
- Free International Debit Card
- Joint holding allowed (with another NRI/PIO, or with a resident relative on a 'Former or Survivor' basis)
- Can be used to make investments in India (mutual funds, real estate)

### Benefits

- **Tax Exempt**: Interest income earned on the NRE account is exempt from Income Tax in India.
- **Flexibility**: Easily transfer funds back to your country of residence without restrictions.
- **Convenience**: Manage your Indian expenses and investments seamlessly through internet banking.

---

## Eligibility

| Criterion | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Residency Status | Must be a Non-Resident Indian (NRI) or Person of Indian Origin (PIO) / Overseas Citizen of India (OCI) |
| Documentation | Valid passport, proof of NRI status (visa/residence permit), and overseas OVD (Officially Valid Document) |
| Joint Holding | Permitted with other NRIs/PIOs. Permitted with resident close relative (as defined in Companies Act) strictly on a "Former or Survivor" basis. |

## Fees and Charges
The NRE account has a specific Minimum Average Balance (MAB) requirement. Non-maintenance attracts charges. See [Account Charges](../charges/account-charges.md) for full details.

---

## Repatriation Rules
The balances held in the NRE account (both principal and interest) are freely and fully repatriable outside India without any monetary limits or prior RBI approval. Funds can also be freely transferred to another NRE or FCNR account.

---

## Tax Implications
Interest earned on NRE Savings and NRE Fixed Deposits is completely exempt from Income Tax in India under Section 10(4)(ii) of the Income Tax Act. Wealth tax is also not applicable.

---

## How to Apply
NRIs can apply online through the Bank's website. The application form and KYC documents must be printed, signed, and attested by the Indian Embassy/Notary/Banker overseas, and couriered to the Bank in India.

---

## Account Redesignation on Return to India
Upon the account holder's return to India for permanent settlement (losing NRI status), the NRE account must be immediately redesignated as a Resident Foreign Currency (RFC) account or a regular Resident Savings Account. It is the customer's responsibility to inform the bank of their change in residency status.

---

## Important Information
Only funds originating from abroad in a permitted foreign currency, or transferred from another NRE/FCNR account, can be deposited into an NRE account. Local Indian Rupee earnings (like rent or dividends in India) cannot be deposited here; they must be deposited in an NRO account.
