# Prepaid Card

## Overview

A Prepaid Card is a payment card that requires funds to be loaded in advance before use. Unlike a debit card, it does not need to be linked to a core banking account (Savings/Current), making it an excellent tool for budgeting, gifting, or providing funds to dependents. 

---

## Features and Benefits

### Usage Channels
Prepaid cards can be used across various channels:
- **Point of Sale (POS):** Swipe or insert the chip at retail stores.
- **Online (E-commerce):** Secure online shopping authenticated via OTP.
- **Contactless:** "Tap to Pay" for fast checkout up to standard limits.
- **ATM Withdrawals:** Supported on select reloadable variants (gift cards typically do not allow cash withdrawals).

### Control and Security
- Limits the risk of fraud since the maximum exposure is limited to the loaded amount.
- Customers can track balances and mini-statements via the Mobile Banking app.

---

## Card Types

1. **Gift Cards:** Single-load, non-reloadable cards pre-funded with a specific amount. Cannot be used for ATM cash withdrawals.
2. **Reloadable Prepaid Cards:** Can be topped up multiple times by the purchaser. Ideal for students or unbanked individuals.
3. **Corporate/Meal Cards:** Pre-loaded by employers for specific use cases (e.g., meal allowances), with merchant category restrictions.

---

## Eligibility and Application

- **Purchaser:** Must have complete KYC. An active account with the bank is preferred but not strictly mandatory (full KYC required for reloadable cards).
- **Beneficiary:** Can be any individual. Minors can use prepaid cards under guardian supervision.
- **Application:** Available over-the-counter at branches or via Internet Banking (virtual variants).

---

## How to Load and Reload

- **Loading:** Funds are loaded at the time of purchase via cash (up to ₹50,000) or account transfer.
- **Reloading:** For reloadable variants, funds can be added instantly using Internet Banking, Mobile Banking, or by depositing cash at a branch.

---

## Fees and Charges

Prepaid cards typically incur an issuance fee and a nominal reload fee (if reloaded at a branch).
Please refer to the [Card Charges](../charges/card-charges.md) schedule for detailed pricing.

---

## Lost/Stolen and Security

- **Blocking:** If lost, the card can be blocked immediately via the customer care helpline.
- **Replacement:** A new card can be issued with the remaining balance transferred, subject to a replacement fee.
- **Authentication:** Protected by a 4-digit PIN for POS/ATM and OTP for online transactions.
