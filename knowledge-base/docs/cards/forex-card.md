# Forex Card

## Overview

A Forex Card (Foreign Exchange Card) is a multi-currency prepaid card designed specifically for international travel. It allows customers to load foreign currencies in advance, protecting them against currency fluctuations and avoiding steep dynamic currency conversion (DCC) and forex markup charges typically associated with standard debit or credit cards.

---

## Features and Benefits

- **Locked-in Exchange Rates:** The exchange rate is locked at the time of loading, protecting the cardholder from market volatility.
- **Multi-Currency Support:** Can hold multiple currencies on a single card (e.g., USD, EUR, GBP, SGD, AUD) and auto-selects the correct currency wallet based on the transaction country.
- **Worldwide Acceptance:** Accepted at millions of ATMs and POS terminals globally via Visa or Mastercard networks.
- **Safety:** More secure than carrying large amounts of physical cash. Unused funds can be easily refunded (encashed) upon return.

---

## Supported Currencies

The Forex Card supports up to <!-- BANK-SPECIFIC: 15 --> major global currencies.
If a transaction is made in a currency not loaded on the card (or if a specific currency wallet has insufficient balance), the transaction will be processed by deducting funds from the default wallet (usually USD) and applying a cross-currency markup.

---

## Loading and Reloading

- **Initial Load:** Must be done at a branch by submitting the application form along with a copy of the Passport, Visa, and flight tickets.
- **Reloading:** Can be done instantly via Internet Banking while abroad, or by an authorized representative at a domestic branch.
- **Limits:** Loading is subject to the annual LRS (Liberalised Remittance Scheme) limit of <!-- BANK-SPECIFIC: USD 250,000 --> per financial year.

---

## Exchange Rates

The exchange rate applied when loading or refunding funds on the Forex Card is the bank's daily Card Rate.
Current exchange rates can be viewed on the bank's official Forex portal.

---

## Fees and Charges

Forex cards typically carry an issuance fee, a reload fee (if done at a branch), and ATM withdrawal charges abroad.
Please refer to the [Card Charges](../charges/card-charges.md) schedule for detailed pricing.

---

## FEMA Guidelines and Compliance

Forex Cards are strictly governed by the Foreign Exchange Management Act (FEMA).
- **Usage:** Strictly for personal or business travel expenses abroad. Cannot be used for prohibited transactions (e.g., forex trading, lottery, gambling).
- **Refunds:** Unspent forex exceeding USD 2,000 must be surrendered/refunded within 180 days of return to India.
- **Nepal & Bhutan:** Forex cards cannot be used for transactions in Nepal and Bhutan.

---

## Lost/Stolen Card

- **Emergency Cash:** If the card is lost abroad, the bank may offer an emergency cash delivery service in major global cities.
- **Replacement:** A backup card is usually provided in the welcome kit, which can be instantly activated via the customer care helpline.
- **Blocking:** The lost card must be immediately blocked via Mobile Banking or Phone Banking to prevent fraud.
