# Credit Card

## Overview

A Credit Card is a financial instrument that allows customers to borrow funds up to a pre-approved credit limit to pay for goods and services. It provides a convenient, cashless payment method with an interest-free grace period on retail purchases, along with rewards, cashback, and travel benefits.

---

## Features and Benefits

### Usage Channels
Credit cards can be used globally across various channels:
- **Point of Sale (POS):** Swipe, dip (chip), or tap (contactless) at physical merchant outlets.
- **Online (E-commerce):** Secure online payments authenticated via OTP or tokenization.
- **ATM:** Cash withdrawals (Note: Cash advances attract immediate finance charges).
- **International Usage:** Can be enabled for cross-border transactions (subject to forex markup).

### Contactless Payments
All credit cards are equipped with NFC (Near Field Communication) technology, allowing "Tap to Pay" for transactions up to <!-- BANK-SPECIFIC: ₹5000 --> without a PIN.

### Reward Programme
Customers earn Reward Points or Cashback on eligible retail spends.
- Points can be redeemed for flights, hotels, merchandise, or statement credit.
- **Exclusions:** Transactions such as fuel purchases, EMI conversions, wallet loads, and cash advances typically do not earn rewards.

### EMI on Credit Card
Cardholders can convert large retail purchases (above a threshold, e.g., <!-- BANK-SPECIFIC: ₹2500 -->) into Equated Monthly Installments (EMIs) at attractive interest rates. Processing fees apply.

### Add-on Cards
Primary cardholders can apply for supplementary (add-on) cards for immediate family members. The credit limit is shared between the primary and add-on cards.

## Card Limits

Card limits are dynamically assigned based on the applicant's credit profile and income.
- **Total Credit Limit:** The maximum outstanding balance allowed on the card.
- **Cash Advance Limit:** A subset of the total limit (usually <!-- BANK-SPECIFIC: 20% to 40% -->) that can be withdrawn as cash.
- **Transaction Limits:** Customers can independently set daily limits for POS, Online, and Contactless transactions via Mobile Banking.

---

## Billing and Payment

### The Billing Cycle
- **Statement Date:** The specific date each month when the bill is generated, summarizing all transactions in the billing cycle.
- **Payment Due Date:** The date by which the payment must reach the bank (typically 15-20 days after the Statement Date).
- **Grace Period:** Enjoy up to <!-- BANK-SPECIFIC: 48 days --> of interest-free credit on retail purchases, provided the previous month's Total Amount Due was paid in full.

### Payment Amounts
- **Total Amount Due (TAD):** The entire outstanding balance.
- **Minimum Amount Due (MAD):** A fraction of the TAD (usually <!-- BANK-SPECIFIC: 5% -->). Paying only the MAD avoids late fees but attracts finance charges on the remaining balance.

### Default and Late Payment
Failure to pay at least the MAD by the due date results in Late Payment Fees and immediate suspension of the card. Persistent defaults will severely impact the customer's CIBIL score and trigger recovery proceedings.

## Security Features

- **EMV Chip & PIN:** Protects against skimming and cloning at physical terminals.
- **Tokenization:** Replaces the actual card number with a digital token for secure online and mobile wallet payments (e.g., Apple Pay, Google Pay).
- **Card Controls:** Customers can instantly freeze/unfreeze their card, or block international/online usage via the Mobile Banking app.

---

## Lost or Stolen Card

In case of loss or theft, the customer must immediately block the card to prevent unauthorized transactions.
1. Block via Mobile Banking, Internet Banking, or the 24/7 Phone Banking helpline.
2. A replacement card will be dispatched and a new PIN must be generated upon activation.
3. As per RBI guidelines on limiting liability, zero liability applies for fraudulent transactions reported immediately.

---

## Disputes and Chargebacks

If a customer spots an unauthorized transaction, duplicate charge, or fails to receive goods paid for, they can raise a dispute.
- **Timeline:** Must be raised within <!-- BANK-SPECIFIC: 30 days --> of the statement date.
- **Temporary Credit:** May be provided during the investigation.
- If the dispute is ruled in the merchant's favor, the temporary credit is reversed along with applicable finance charges.
