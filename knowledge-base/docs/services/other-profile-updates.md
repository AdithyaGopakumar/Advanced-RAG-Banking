# Other Profile Updates

## Overview

Customers can keep their bank records current by updating their Email ID, PAN, Name, and Date of Birth. While some updates are simple and can be done digitally, sensitive changes like a Name Correction require a physical branch visit and legal documentation.

---

## 1. Updating Email ID

Adding or updating an Email ID ensures you receive timely e-statements, transaction alerts, and OTPs for certain online transactions.

- **Via Internet/Mobile Banking (Instant):** Navigate to `Profile > Update Email`. Enter the new email ID. You will receive an OTP on your registered mobile number, and often a confirmation link to the new email ID.
- **Via Branch:** Fill out the Customer Profile Update form. It takes 1-2 working days to process.

---

## 2. Updating / Linking PAN

A valid Permanent Account Number (PAN) is mandatory for cash deposits exceeding ₹50,000, fixed deposits exceeding ₹50,000, and for claiming TDS exemptions (Form 15G/15H).

- **Via Internet/Mobile Banking:** Navigate to `Services > Update PAN`. Enter your 10-digit PAN. The system will instantly verify it with the NSDL database. Once verified, it is linked immediately.
- **Via Branch:** Submit a self-attested photocopy of the PAN card along with the profile update form.

---

## 3. Name Change

A change in name (e.g., due to marriage, divorce, or legal change) **cannot** be done online.

- **Required Action:** Visit your Home Branch.
- **Required Documents:** 
  - **For Marriage:** Marriage Certificate or a notarized joint affidavit, plus an updated OVD (like Aadhaar) showing the new name.
  - **For Legal Change:** Gazette notification of the name change and an updated OVD.
- **Processing Time:** Once verified by the branch manager, the name is updated in the core banking system within 2 to 3 working days. You will need to request a new Debit Card and Cheque Book bearing the new name.

---

## 4. Date of Birth (DOB) Correction

If your date of birth is incorrectly recorded, it can affect your eligibility for Senior Citizen deposit rates or Minor account status.

- **Required Action:** Visit your Home Branch.
- **Required Documents:** Provide a valid proof of age (e.g., Birth Certificate, Passport, or Aadhaar Card).
- **Processing Time:** Processed within 1 to 2 working days after physical document verification.
