# Account Closure

## Overview

Customers can choose to close their savings or current accounts at any time. To ensure security and prevent unauthorized closures, account closure usually requires a physical visit to the home branch or a formal written request, though some zero-balance digital accounts can be closed online.

---

## What You Need Before You Start

Before submitting a closure request, ensure you have completed the following:
1. **Clear Dues:** Settle any negative balances, pending loan EMIs, or pending charges on the account.
2. **Delink Mandates:** Cancel any active NACH mandates, SIPs, or standing instructions linked to the account.
3. **Surrender Materials:** Keep your debit card(s) and unused cheque leaves ready for surrender.
4. **Empty Balance:** Transfer the remaining balance out, or decide how you want it settled (Cash payout up to limits, DD, or transfer to another bank).

---

## How to Submit a Request

### At the Branch (Standard Method)
1. Visit your **Home Branch**.
2. Request and fill out the **Account Closure Form**.
3. All joint account holders must sign the form if the account operating mandate is "Jointly".
4. Surrender your physical Debit Card (it will be cut across the magnetic stripe/chip by the teller) and unused cheque leaves.
5. Provide the details of the destination account where the final balance should be transferred.

### Via Digital Channels
*Currently, full KYC branch-opened accounts cannot be closed digitally due to regulatory security reasons. However, limited-KYC digital accounts can sometimes be closed via the Mobile Banking app by navigating to `Services > Account Management > Close Account`.*

---

## Processing Time and Outcome

- **Timeline:** Once the signed form and materials are submitted, the account is usually closed within **1 to 3 working days**.
- **Confirmation:** You will receive a final SMS/Email confirmation that the account has been closed.
- **Access Revoked:** Upon closure, you will no longer be able to access the account via Internet or Mobile Banking.

---

## Charges

- Closing an account within 14 days of opening is usually **Free**.
- Closing an account between 14 days and 1 year of opening attracts an **Account Closure Charge**.
- Closing an account after 1 year of opening is generally **Free**.
- Please refer to [Account Charges](../charges/account-charges.md) for the exact fee amounts.
