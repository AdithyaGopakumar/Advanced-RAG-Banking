# Deceased Customer Claims

## Overview

In the unfortunate event of an account holder's demise, the bank follows a structured process to settle the funds to the rightful claimant. The complexity of the process depends entirely on whether a Nominee was registered, or if the account was held jointly with a survivorship mandate.

---

## Immediate Action Required

When an account holder passes away, a family member or representative should immediately notify the Home Branch in writing, providing a copy of the **Death Certificate**.
- This allows the bank to freeze the account (preventing unauthorized debit transactions).
- Recurring mandates (SIPs, utility bills) and outstanding loan EMIs will be paused pending settlement.

---

## Settlement Scenarios

### Scenario 1: Claim by Registered Nominee (Simplest)
If the deceased had registered a nominee, the bank will settle the claim directly to the nominee.
**Required Documents:**
1. Death Certificate (Original for verification + Copy).
2. Deceased Claim Form signed by the Nominee.
3. Nominee's KYC documents (PAN and Aadhaar).
*Note: The nominee receives the funds as a trustee of the legal heirs.*

### Scenario 2: Joint Account with Survivorship Mandate
If the account was held jointly (e.g., "Either or Survivor" or "Former or Survivor"), the surviving account holder(s) retain the right to the funds.
**Required Documents:**
1. Death Certificate.
2. Request form from the survivor(s) to remove the deceased's name from the account.
3. The account continues in the name of the survivor(s).

### Scenario 3: No Nominee and No Survivor (Legal Heir Claim)
If there is no nominee and the account was held singly (or if all joint holders are deceased), the claim must be settled in favor of the legal heirs.
**Required Documents:**
1. Death Certificate.
2. Deceased Claim Form signed by all legal heirs.
3. KYC of all legal heirs.
4. **For smaller balances (e.g., below ₹5 Lakhs):** An Indemnity Bond with surety may suffice, depending on bank policy.
5. **For larger balances:** A Succession Certificate from a competent court, or a Letter of Administration, is legally mandatory.

---

## Processing Time

As per RBI guidelines, once all required documents are submitted to the satisfaction of the bank, the claim must be settled within **15 days**.

---

## Important Rules Regarding Lockers

If the deceased held a Safe Deposit Locker:
- **With Nominee:** The bank will grant access to the nominee to remove the contents. The bank will prepare an inventory of the items removed in the presence of witnesses.
- **Without Nominee:** Legal heirs must provide a succession certificate to gain access.
