# Statement Requests

## Overview

An account statement is a detailed record of all transactions (credits and debits) that have occurred in a bank account over a specific period. Customers can access their statements digitally for free or request physical copies at a branch.

---

## How to Get a Statement

### 1. Via Mobile Banking App (Instant & Free)
- Navigate to `Accounts > View Statement`.
- Select the date range (e.g., Last 1 Month, Last 6 Months, or Custom Dates).
- Tap **Download PDF** or **Email Statement**.
- *Note:* The PDF is usually password-protected (the password format is typically a combination of the account holder's name and DOB).

### 2. Via Internet Banking (Instant & Free)
- Log in and navigate to `Accounts > Account Statement`.
- Select the account and the required date range.
- You can view it directly on the screen or download it in PDF or Excel (XLS) formats.

### 3. Via WhatsApp Banking (Instant & Free)
- Send a message (e.g., "Statement") to the bank's official verified WhatsApp number from your registered mobile number.
- Follow the prompt to receive a mini-statement or a recent 1-month statement as a PDF.

### 4. Via Branch (Physical Copy)
- Visit any branch and request a physical printout of your statement.
- **Charges:** Physical statements requested at the branch incur a per-page printing fee. See [Service Charges](../charges/service-charges.md) for details.

### 5. Via ATM (Mini Statement)
- Visit any ATM, insert your debit card, and enter your PIN.
- Select **Mini Statement**.
- The ATM will print a receipt showing your last 5 to 10 transactions and your current balance.

---

## E-Statement Registration

Customers can opt to receive their account statements automatically via email at the end of every month.
1. **Registration:** Log in to Internet Banking and navigate to `Services > Register for E-Statement`.
2. **Frequency:** Select Monthly, Quarterly, or Annually.
3. **Benefits:** E-statements are free, environmentally friendly, and serve as valid address/income proof for most official purposes.
