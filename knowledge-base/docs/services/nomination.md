# Nomination

## Overview

Nomination is a facility that enables an account holder or deposit holder to nominate an individual to whom the bank will pay the balances in the event of the account holder's death. The RBI actively encourages all customers to register a nominee to ensure a smooth transfer of funds to their loved ones without requiring complex legal documentation.

---

## Why Nomination Matters

- **Avoids Legal Hassles:** Without a nominee, the legal heirs must produce a Succession Certificate or Letter of Administration from a court, which is a time-consuming and expensive process.
- **Trustee Status:** The nominee receives the funds from the bank as a *Trustee* of the legal heirs, not necessarily as the absolute owner. The bank's liability ends once the funds are handed over to the nominee.
- **Mandatory Opt-out:** If a customer does not want to nominate anyone, they must sign a specific "Opt-out" declaration form.

---

## Nomination Rules

- **Number of Nominees:** 
  - **Bank Accounts and Deposits:** Only **one** individual can be nominated per account or deposit.
  - **Locker Facility:** Some locker types held jointly may allow up to two nominees.
- **Minor Nominees:** A minor can be appointed as a nominee. However, the account holder must designate a major (adult) as the appointee to receive the funds on the minor's behalf in the event of a claim.
- **Joint Accounts:** The nomination must be signed by *all* joint account holders.

---

## How to Register or Change a Nominee

### 1. Via Internet Banking / Mobile Banking (For Single Accounts)
1. Log in to your digital banking portal.
2. Navigate to `Services > Nominee Details`.
3. Select the specific account or deposit.
4. Add the nominee's details (Name, Relationship, DOB, and Address).
5. Authenticate with an OTP. The nomination is updated instantly.

### 2. At the Branch (Required for Joint Accounts)
1. Visit your Home Branch.
2. Fill out the relevant form:
   - **Form DA1:** To register a *new* nominee.
   - **Form DA2:** To *cancel* an existing nomination.
   - **Form DA3:** To *modify* (change) an existing nominee.
3. For joint accounts, **all account holders must sign the form**.
4. Submit the form to the branch official. It will be processed within 1 to 2 working days.

---

## Fees and Charges

- Registering a nominee for the first time is completely **Free**.
- Modifying or cancelling a nominee may attract a nominal charge (refer to [Service Charges](../charges/service-charges.md)).
