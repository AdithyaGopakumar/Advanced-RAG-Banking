# Customer Certificates

## Overview

Customers often require official bank certificates for tax filing, visa applications, or auditing purposes. The bank issues several standardized certificates, most of which can be downloaded instantly for free via digital channels.

---

## Types of Certificates

### 1. Interest Certificate
- **Purpose:** Used for filing Income Tax Returns (ITR). It shows the total interest earned on Savings Accounts and Fixed/Recurring Deposits during a financial year, as well as the interest paid on Loans.
- **How to Get It:** 
  - Log in to Internet Banking.
  - Navigate to `Services > Certificates > Interest Certificate`.
  - Select the Financial Year and download the PDF.
- **Charges:** Free via digital channels.

### 2. TDS Certificate (Form 16A)
- **Purpose:** Issued if the bank has deducted Tax Deducted at Source (TDS) on your deposit interest. It serves as proof of tax paid.
- **How to Get It:** 
  - Form 16A is generated quarterly.
  - Navigate to `Services > Tax Services > Form 16A` in Internet Banking.
  - Download the PDF.
- **Charges:** Free via digital channels.

### 3. Balance Certificate
- **Purpose:** An official letter stating the exact balance in the customer's account on a specific date. Often required by embassies for Visa applications.
- **How to Get It:** 
  - Balance certificates usually require a physical bank seal and signature to be accepted by embassies.
  - Visit the Home Branch and submit a written request.
- **Charges:** Issuance of a physical balance certificate attracts a fee. Refer to [Service Charges](../charges/service-charges.md).

### 4. Loan Statement and Provisional Certificate
- **Purpose:** Shows the principal and interest breakdown of a loan, required for claiming Section 80C and 24(b) tax deductions.
- **How to Get It:** 
  - Log in to Internet or Mobile Banking.
  - Navigate to `Loans > Loan Statement / Provisional IT Certificate`.
  - Download the PDF.
- **Charges:** Free via digital channels.

---

## Important Notes

- **Digital Signatures:** Certificates downloaded via Internet Banking are digitally signed and are legally valid for tax purposes without a physical stamp.
- **Password Protection:** Downloaded certificates are usually encrypted. The password format is mentioned on the download screen (typically a combination of PAN or DOB).
