# NRO Fixed Deposit

## Overview

The Non-Resident Ordinary (NRO) Fixed Deposit allows Non-Resident Indians (NRIs) and Persons of Indian Origin (PIOs) to invest their Indian earnings (such as rent, pension, or dividends) in a secure, Rupee-denominated term deposit in India.

---

## Features and Benefits

### Key features

- Rupee-denominated term deposit maintained in India
- Funded using Indian earnings or remittances from abroad
- Flexible tenure options ranging from 7 days to 10 years
- Joint holding allowed with another NRI or a Resident Indian (on a "Former or Survivor" basis)
- Auto-renewal facility available

### Benefits

- **Growth on Local Income**: The ideal way to earn high returns on income generated within India.
- **Convenience**: Manage the deposit through Internet Banking alongside your NRO savings account.
- **Loan Facility**: Avail a loan or overdraft against the NRO deposit.

---

## Eligibility

| Criterion | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Residency Status | Must be a Non-Resident Indian (NRI), Person of Indian Origin (PIO), or Overseas Citizen of India (OCI). |
| Source of Funds | Can be funded from legitimate Indian earnings or foreign remittances. |
| Tenure | Minimum 7 days to Maximum 10 years. |

---

## Interest Rates

NRO Fixed Deposits earn competitive interest rates similar to domestic fixed deposits. See [Deposit Interest Rates](../interest-rates/deposit-interest-rates.md). Note that senior citizen premium rates are not applicable to NRO deposits.

---

## Repatriation Rules

The principal and interest of an NRO deposit are **not freely repatriable**. However, NRIs can repatriate up to USD 1 Million per financial year out of NRO balances, subject to providing necessary tax paid certificates (Form 15CA/15CB) from a Chartered Accountant.

---

## Premature Withdrawal

Premature withdrawal is permitted, subject to a penalty on the applicable interest rate for the period the deposit was held. See [Account Charges](../charges/account-charges.md) for current penalty rates.

---

## Tax Implications (TDS)

Interest earned on an NRO Fixed Deposit is subject to Tax Deducted at Source (TDS) at the rate of 30% (plus applicable surcharge and cess). However, NRIs can avail of a lower TDS rate if their country of residence has a Double Taxation Avoidance Agreement (DTAA) with India, by submitting the required TRC and Form 10F.

---

## How to Open

Existing NRI customers can easily open an NRO FD by transferring funds from their NRO Savings Account using Internet Banking. New customers must submit the [Account Opening Documents](../forms/account-opening-documents.md) for NRI accounts.
