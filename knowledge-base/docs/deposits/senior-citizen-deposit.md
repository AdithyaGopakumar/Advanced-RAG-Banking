# Senior Citizen Fixed Deposit

## Overview

The Senior Citizen Fixed Deposit is a special term deposit tailored for elderly individuals (aged 60 and above). It offers all the benefits of a regular Fixed Deposit along with a premium interest rate, providing a higher yield to support post-retirement income.

---

## Features and Benefits

### Key features

- Premium interest rate over the standard FD rate
- Flexible tenure options from 7 days to 10 years
- Interest payout options: Cumulative or Non-cumulative (monthly/quarterly) for regular income
- Auto-renewal facility available
- Overdraft/Loan facility against the deposit value

### Benefits

- **Higher Returns**: Specially designed higher interest rate to maximize savings.
- **Regular Income**: Opt for monthly interest payouts to cover regular living expenses.
- **Capital Safety**: Zero market risk, ensuring complete peace of mind.

## Interest Rates

Senior citizens earn an additional premium (e.g., 0.50% p.a.) over the regular fixed deposit rates. See [Deposit Interest Rates](../interest-rates/deposit-interest-rates.md) for the exact premium and current rates.

---

## Premature Withdrawal

Premature withdrawal is permitted. A penalty is typically levied on the applicable interest rate for the period the deposit was held. See [Account Charges](../charges/account-charges.md) for current penalty details.

---

## Renewal Options

Customers can opt for auto-renewal. At maturity, the deposit is automatically renewed for the same tenure at the prevailing Senior Citizen interest rate on the date of renewal.

---

## Tax Implications

Interest earned is taxable. However, under Section 80TTB of the Income Tax Act, senior citizens can claim a deduction of up to ₹50,000 on interest income. TDS is not deducted if the total interest in a financial year is below ₹50,000, or if a valid Form 15H is submitted.

---

## How to Open

Existing senior citizen customers can instantly open the deposit via Internet Banking or Mobile Banking. New customers can visit a branch with their age proof (e.g., PAN, Aadhaar) and submit the [Account Opening Documents](../forms/account-opening-documents.md).
