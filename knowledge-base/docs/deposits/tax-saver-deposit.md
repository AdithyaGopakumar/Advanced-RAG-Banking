# Tax Saver Fixed Deposit

## Overview

The Tax Saver Fixed Deposit is a specialized term deposit designed to help customers save on Income Tax under Section 80C of the Income Tax Act, 1961. It comes with a mandatory lock-in period but offers guaranteed returns along with tax benefits.

---

## Features and Benefits

### Key Features
- Mandatory lock-in period of 5 years
- Maximum deposit limit of ₹1.5 Lakhs per financial year
- Available in both cumulative and non-cumulative interest payout options
- Cannot be withdrawn prematurely
- Cannot be pledged as collateral for a loan

### Benefits
- **Tax Savings**: Claim deductions up to ₹1.5 Lakhs under Section 80C.
- **Assured Returns**: Fixed interest rate for the entire 5-year tenure.

---

## Eligibility

Available only to Resident Individuals and Hindu Undivided Families (HUFs). NRIs, companies, and firms are not eligible to open a Tax Saver FD.

---

## Interest Rates

The interest rate is fixed at the time of opening and aligns with the Bank's standard 5-year FD rates. See [Deposit Interest Rates](../interest-rates/deposit-interest-rates.md). Senior citizens receive an additional premium.

---

## Tax Benefits

The principal amount deposited (up to ₹1.5 Lakhs in a financial year) is eligible for a tax deduction under Section 80C. However, the interest earned on this deposit is fully taxable according to the depositor's income slab, and TDS applies unless a valid Form 15G/15H is provided.

---

## Lock-In Period

The Tax Saver Deposit has a strict statutory lock-in period of **5 years**. Premature withdrawal is strictly not permitted under any circumstances before the completion of 5 years, except in the event of the primary account holder's death.

---

## How to Open

Customers can open this deposit via Internet Banking or by visiting a branch. A PAN card is mandatory for opening a Tax Saver Deposit.
