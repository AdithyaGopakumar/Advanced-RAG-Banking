# NRE Fixed Deposit

## Overview

The Non-Resident External (NRE) Fixed Deposit is a Rupee-denominated term deposit that allows Non-Resident Indians (NRIs) and Persons of Indian Origin (PIOs) to invest their foreign earnings securely in India. It offers guaranteed returns that are completely tax-free and fully repatriable.

---

## Features and Benefits

### Key features

- Rupee-denominated deposit held in India
- Principal and interest are fully repatriable without restrictions
- Flexible tenure options typically ranging from 1 year to 10 years
- Joint holding allowed with another NRI/PIO, or with a resident relative on a "Former or Survivor" basis
- Auto-renewal facility available

### Benefits

- **Tax Exempt**: Interest earned on NRE Fixed Deposits is exempt from Income Tax in India.
- **High Yield**: Earn competitive interest rates compared to global deposit rates.
- **Convenience**: Manage the deposit seamlessly through Internet Banking from anywhere in the world.

---

## Eligibility

| Criterion | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Residency Status | Must be a Non-Resident Indian (NRI), Person of Indian Origin (PIO), or Overseas Citizen of India (OCI). |
| Source of Funds | Funds must be remitted from abroad in a permitted foreign currency or transferred from an existing NRE/FCNR account. |
| Tenure | Minimum 1 year to Maximum 10 years (as per RBI guidelines for NRE deposits). |

---

## Interest Rates

NRE Fixed Deposits earn competitive interest rates. See [Deposit Interest Rates](../interest-rates/deposit-interest-rates.md) for current NRE deposit rates. Note that senior citizen premium rates are not applicable to NRE deposits.

---

## Repatriation Rules

The entire principal amount and the interest earned are freely and fully repatriable outside India. No prior approval from RBI is required. Funds can also be freely transferred to another NRE or FCNR account.

---

## Premature Withdrawal

Premature withdrawal is allowed subject to a penalty. However, as per RBI guidelines, if an NRE deposit is withdrawn before completion of the minimum 1-year tenure, **no interest** is payable. See [Account Charges](../charges/account-charges.md) for penalty details.

---

## Tax Implications

Interest earned on NRE Fixed Deposits is completely exempt from Income Tax in India under Section 10(4)(ii) of the Income Tax Act. No TDS is deducted. Wealth tax is also not applicable.

---

## How to Open

Existing NRI customers can open an NRE FD instantly by funding it from their NRE Savings Account via Internet Banking. New customers must submit the required [Account Opening Documents](../forms/account-opening-documents.md) for NRI accounts.
