# Gold Loan

## Overview

The Gold Loan is a secured, short-term credit facility provided against the pledge of gold ornaments or coins. It is disbursed instantly with minimal documentation and offers flexible repayment options.

---

## Key Features

| Feature | Details |
|---|---|
| Maximum Loan Amount | Up to <!-- BANK-SPECIFIC: ₹50 Lakhs --> (Subject to gold valuation and LTV limits). |
| Maximum Tenure | Typically <!-- BANK-SPECIFIC: 12 to 36 Months -->. |
| Interest Rate Type | Fixed. |
| Repayment Mode | Monthly interest with bullet principal repayment, or regular EMI. |
| Collateral Required | Pledge of physical gold ornaments/coins (minimum 18-karat purity). |

---

## Eligibility

Eligibility is extremely straightforward as the loan is fully secured by liquid collateral.

| Criterion | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Age | Minimum 18 years to Maximum 75 years. |
| Income Proof | Usually not required. |
| Credit Score | Not a primary deciding factor. |
| Collateral Purity | Gold items must be between 18K and 24K purity. |

---

## Gold Valuation
The loan amount is determined by the Loan-to-Value (LTV) ratio set by the RBI, which is currently capped at <!-- BANK-SPECIFIC: 75% --> of the appraised value of the 22-karat gold equivalent.

---

## Interest Rates

Interest rates are generally lower than personal loans due to the secured nature of the product. Rates are fixed for the tenure of the loan. See [Loan Interest Rates](../interest-rates/loan-interest-rates.md).

---

## Fees and Charges

A nominal valuation fee is charged by the appraiser. Processing fees are generally low. See [Loan Charges](../charges/loan-charges.md).

---

## Required Documents

Only basic KYC (Identity and OVD (Officially Valid Document)) is required. Income proof is not required. See [Loan Application Documents](../forms/loan-application-documents.md).

---

## Prepayment and Foreclosure

Customers can prepay or foreclose their Gold Loan at any point without any penalties. This provides maximum flexibility for short-term liquidity needs.

---

## Default

If the borrower fails to repay the loan by the maturity date or after the grace period, the bank will issue a notice and subsequently auction the pledged gold ornaments to recover the principal, interest, and auction charges.

---

## Closure and NOC

Once the outstanding dues (principal + interest) are cleared, the pledged gold is immediately handed back to the customer. No formal NOC is typically required.

---

## Tax Benefits

There are no direct tax benefits associated with a Gold Loan.

---

## Co-applicant and Guarantor

Gold Loans are individual loans and do not require co-applicants or guarantors. The physical gold pledge serves as complete security.

---

## Repayment and Gold Release

Repayment can be made via cash, cheque, or online transfer. Once the loan is fully repaid, the pledged gold is returned immediately at the branch.

---

## How to Apply

Visit the nearest branch with your gold ornaments and valid KYC documents. The appraisal and disbursement process can be completed in under an hour.
