# Business Loan

## Overview

The Business Loan provides working capital and term financing to MSMEs, self-employed professionals, and non-professionals to expand their business operations. It can be secured or unsecured depending on the loan amount and borrower profile.

---

## Key Features

| Feature | Details |
|---|---|
| Maximum Loan Amount | Up to <!-- BANK-SPECIFIC: ₹50 Lakhs --> (unsecured) or higher (secured). |
| Maximum Tenure | Up to <!-- BANK-SPECIFIC: 48 to 60 Months -->. |
| Interest Rate Type | Fixed or Floating (Repo/MCLR linked). |
| Repayment Mode | Monthly EMI. |
| Collateral Required | None for lower limits. Property/Fixed Assets for higher limits. |

| Criterion | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Age | Minimum 21 years to Maximum 65 years. |
| Business Vintage | Minimum 3 years of continuous operation. |
| Turnover | Minimum Annual Turnover of <!-- BANK-SPECIFIC: ₹10 Lakhs -->. |
| Credit Score | Minimum CIBIL/CMR score of <!-- BANK-SPECIFIC: 700 -->. |

---

## Interest Rates

Rates vary significantly based on the borrower's credit rating, business vintage, and whether collateral is provided. See [Loan Interest Rates](../interest-rates/loan-interest-rates.md).

---

## Fees and Charges

Standard processing fees apply. Prepayment is allowed but often attracts foreclosure charges unless specific waivers apply. See [Loan Charges](../charges/loan-charges.md).

---

## Required Documents

Requires business entity proofs (GST, Udyam), promoter KYC, and audited financials. See [Loan Application Documents](../forms/loan-application-documents.md).

---

## Repayment and Prepayment

Repayment is typically done via monthly EMIs. Part-prepayment or full foreclosure is permitted but usually attracts foreclosure charges (e.g., <!-- BANK-SPECIFIC: 2% to 5% --> of the outstanding principal). See [Loan Charges](../charges/loan-charges.md).

---

## Default

Defaults will impact the business's CMR (Credit Market Report) rating and CIBIL score. For secured loans, the bank may initiate proceedings under the SARFAESI Act to recover dues from the pledged collateral.

---

## Closure and NOC

Upon full repayment, an NOC is issued. If the loan was secured, the property documents or fixed assets' charge is released and a charge satisfaction form is filed with the ROC (if applicable).

---

## Tax Benefits

Interest paid on a Business Loan is fully tax-deductible as a business expense under the Income Tax Act. This helps reduce the net taxable income of the business.

---

## Security and Collateral

Business loans can be **Unsecured** (up to a certain limit, based on financials and CMR) or **Secured** (backed by residential/commercial property, machinery, or fixed deposits). Secured loans attract lower interest rates.

---

## How to Apply

Business owners can apply via our Corporate Banking portal, visit a specialized SME branch, or contact their dedicated Relationship Manager. Site visits may be required as part of the assessment process.

---

## Repayment Options

Repayment can be made via EMI, structured installment plans (tailored to business cash flows), or overdraft limit reductions. Interest is calculated on daily reducing balance.
