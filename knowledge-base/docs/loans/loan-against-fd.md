# Loan Against Fixed Deposit

## Overview

The Loan Against Fixed Deposit provides instant liquidity without breaking an existing FD. A lien is marked on the FD, and the borrower receives a demand loan or overdraft facility at a marginally higher interest rate than the FD itself.

---

## Key Features

| Feature | Details |
|---|---|
| Maximum Loan Amount | Up to <!-- BANK-SPECIFIC: 90% to 95% --> of the FD principal value. |
| Tenure | Up to the remaining maturity period of the underlying FD. |
| Interest Rate Type | Fixed (Linked to FD rate). |
| Repayment Mode | Flexible. Interest serviced monthly or upon maturity. |
| Collateral Required | Marking of lien on the Fixed Deposit. |

---

## Eligibility

Available to all domestic and NRI customers (NRE/NRO) holding an active Fixed Deposit with the bank. Tax-saver FDs (in their 5-year lock-in) and FDs held in the name of a minor are generally **not** eligible.

---

## Interest Rates

The interest rate is strictly pegged at a spread above the underlying FD rate (typically FD Rate + <!-- BANK-SPECIFIC: 1% to 2% -->). See [Loan Interest Rates](../interest-rates/loan-interest-rates.md).

---

## Fees and Charges

There are usually zero processing fees and zero prepayment penalties. See [Loan Charges](../charges/loan-charges.md).

---

## Application Process

Customers can avail this facility instantly via Internet Banking or Mobile Banking. The lien is marked digitally, and funds are immediately credited to the savings account. Branch applications require submitting the physical FD receipt properly discharged.

---

## Required Documents

For digital applications, no documents are required. For branch applications, a filled form and the original FD receipt are needed. See [Loan Application Documents](../forms/loan-application-documents.md).

---

## Default

If the overdraft or demand loan is not repaid by the time the underlying Fixed Deposit matures, the bank will automatically liquidate the FD, adjust the outstanding loan amount (principal + interest), and credit the balance to the customer's savings account.

---

## Closure and NOC

Customers can close the loan at any time by paying the outstanding amount. Upon closure, the lien marked on the Fixed Deposit is instantly removed, freeing up the FD for withdrawal or regular maturity.
