# Loan Against Property

## Overview

Loan Against Property (LAP) allows customers to leverage the value of their residential or commercial real estate to secure high-value funding for personal or business needs. It typically offers lower interest rates and longer tenures compared to unsecured personal loans.

---

## Key Features

| Feature | Details |
|---|---|
| Maximum Loan Amount | Up to <!-- BANK-SPECIFIC: ₹5 Crores --> (Subject to LTV and FOIR). |
| Maximum Tenure | Up to <!-- BANK-SPECIFIC: 15 Years -->. |
| Interest Rate Type | Floating (EBLR-linked). |
| Repayment Mode | EMI via Auto-Debit. |
| Collateral Required | Equitable or Registered Mortgage of residential/commercial property. |

---

## Prepayment and Foreclosure

For floating-rate LAP given to individual borrowers, there are zero prepayment and foreclosure charges. For fixed-rate LAP, charges apply as per the [Loan Charges](../charges/loan-charges.md) schedule.

---

## Default

Defaults on LAP are severe. The bank can initiate recovery proceedings under the SARFAESI Act, allowing them to auction the mortgaged property to recover dues. 

---

## Closure and NOC

After full repayment, the bank issues an NOC and releases the original property title documents back to the borrower within <!-- BANK-SPECIFIC: 15 to 30 days -->.

---

## Tax Benefits

Interest paid on a Loan Against Property is tax-deductible under Section 37(1) of the Income Tax Act if the loan is used exclusively for business purposes. If used for personal purposes, no tax benefits are available.

---

## Co-applicant and Guarantor

All co-owners of the property must necessarily be co-applicants to the loan. Guarantors may be required in the case of partnership/corporate borrowers.

---

## Interest Rates

Rates are floating and linked to the repo rate. LAP rates are generally higher than Home Loan rates but lower than Personal Loan rates. See [Loan Interest Rates](../interest-rates/loan-interest-rates.md).

---

## Fees and Charges

Standard processing and valuation fees apply. As per RBI guidelines, individual borrowers on floating rate loans are exempt from prepayment and foreclosure penalties. See [Loan Charges](../charges/loan-charges.md).

---

## Required Documents

Includes KYC, income proofs, and all property chain documents. See [Loan Application Documents](../forms/loan-application-documents.md).

---

## Eligibility

Eligibility is determined by the property value (LTV) and the applicant's repayment capacity (FOIR).

### For salaried individuals

| Criterion | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Age | Minimum 21 years to Maximum 60 years. |
| Minimum Income | <!-- BANK-SPECIFIC: ₹30,000 --> net monthly income. |
| Employment Tenure | Minimum 3 years of work experience. |
| Credit Score (CIBIL) | Minimum <!-- BANK-SPECIFIC: 700 -->. |

### For self-employed individuals

| Criterion | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Age | Minimum 21 years to Maximum 65 years. |
| Minimum Income / Turnover | Minimum Annual Income of <!-- BANK-SPECIFIC: ₹3 Lakhs --> as per ITR. |
| Business Vintage | Minimum 3 years in the same business. |
| Credit Score (CIBIL) | Minimum <!-- BANK-SPECIFIC: 700 -->. |

---

## Property Valuation

The maximum loan amount is determined by a technical valuation of the property conducted by a bank-approved valuer. Typically, the Loan-to-Value (LTV) ratio is capped at <!-- BANK-SPECIFIC: 60% to 70% --> of the market value.
