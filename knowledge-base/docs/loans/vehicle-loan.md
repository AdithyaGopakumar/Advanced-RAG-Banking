# Vehicle Loan

## Overview

The Vehicle Loan provides financing for the purchase of new or pre-owned two-wheelers and four-wheelers. The loan is secured by the hypothecation of the purchased vehicle.

---

| Feature | Details |
|---|---|
| Maximum Loan Amount | Up to <!-- BANK-SPECIFIC: 100% --> of the on-road price for select models. |
| Maximum Tenure | Up to <!-- BANK-SPECIFIC: 7 Years (84 months) --> for new cars. |
| Interest Rate Type | Fixed or Floating. |
| Repayment Mode | EMI via Auto-Debit. |
| Collateral Required | Hypothecation of the vehicle in favor of the bank. |

## Vehicle Types Covered

- New and Pre-owned Cars (up to 5 years old)
- Two-Wheelers and Superbikes
- Electric Vehicles (EVs)
- Commercial Vehicles

---

## Interest Rates

Rates are highly competitive and depend on the vehicle segment (EVs often get special concessions). See [Loan Interest Rates](../interest-rates/loan-interest-rates.md).

---

## Fees and Charges

Standard processing fees apply. Prepayment is allowed (usually after 6 months) subject to a foreclosure penalty if the rate is fixed. See [Loan Charges](../charges/loan-charges.md).

---

## Required Documents

Includes KYC, Income Proof, and the vehicle's Proforma Invoice. For a complete list, refer to [Loan Application Documents](../forms/loan-application-documents.md).

---

## Prepayment and Foreclosure

Part-prepayment and full foreclosure are allowed, usually after a lock-in period of 6 EMIs. For fixed-rate loans, foreclosure charges apply as per the [Loan Charges](../charges/loan-charges.md) schedule.

---

## Default

In the event of continuous default, the bank reserves the right to repossess and auction the hypothecated vehicle to recover the dues, following due regulatory process.

---

## Closure and NOC

Upon full repayment, the bank issues an NOC and Form 35 to cancel the hypothecation from the vehicle's Registration Certificate (RC) at the RTO.

---

## Tax Benefits

Generally, there are no tax benefits for a vehicle loan taken for personal use. If the vehicle is used for business purposes, professionals and business owners can claim depreciation and interest paid as business expenses under the Income Tax Act.

---

## Co-applicant and Guarantor

A co-applicant can be added to increase loan eligibility. A guarantor may be required if the applicant's credit score is borderline or if the loan amount is exceptionally high relative to income.

---

## How to Apply

Applications can be initiated via our mobile app, internet banking portal, or by visiting any of our branches. Required documents must be submitted digitally or in person for verification.

---

## Repayment Options

Repayments are processed through automated ECS/NACH mandates linked to your bank account. Customers can also choose to pay via standing instructions on their savings account.
