# Home Loan

## Overview

The Home Loan product offers financing for the purchase, construction, or renovation of residential properties. It provides long-term, high-value funding secured by the property being financed, with flexible repayment options and tax benefits.

---

## Key Features

The Home Loan is designed for long-term affordability with the following core characteristics:

| Feature | Details |
|---|---|
| Maximum Loan Amount | Up to <!-- BANK-SPECIFIC: ₹X Crores --> (Subject to LTV and FOIR). |
| Maximum Tenure | Up to <!-- BANK-SPECIFIC: 30 Years -->. |
| Interest Rate Type | Floating (linked to Repo Rate) or Fixed. |
| Repayment Mode | Equated Monthly Installment (EMI) via Auto-Debit or NACH. |
| Collateral Required | Equitable Mortgage of the property being financed. |

## Eligibility

Eligibility is determined by age, income, credit history, and the value of the property.

### For salaried individuals

| Criterion | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Age | Minimum 21 years to Maximum 60 years (at loan maturity). |
| Minimum Income | <!-- BANK-SPECIFIC: ₹25,000 --> net monthly income. |
| Employment Tenure | Minimum 2 years of work experience (with at least 6 months in current job). |
| Credit Score (CIBIL) | Minimum <!-- BANK-SPECIFIC: 700 --> for standard rates. |

### For self-employed individuals

| Criterion | Requirement |
|---|---|

## Interest Rates

Home Loans are available at both fixed and floating interest rates. Current applicable rates, spreads, and special concessions (e.g., for female co-applicants) are maintained in the [Loan Interest Rates](../interest-rates/loan-interest-rates.md) document.

## Fees and Charges

Standard charges include Processing Fees, Valuation Charges, and Legal/Title Search fees. 
> [!NOTE]
> As per RBI regulations, there are **no prepayment or foreclosure charges** on floating-rate Home Loans for individual borrowers.

For the exact schedule of fees, refer to the [Loan Charges](../charges/loan-charges.md) document.

---

## Required Documents

The application requires KYC, financial proofs (Salary slips/ITR), and property-specific documents (Sale Deed, NOC, Approved Plan). For the full checklist, refer to [Loan Application Documents](../forms/loan-application-documents.md).

---

## Application Process

1. **Submission**: Submit the application form along with KYC, income proofs, and property documents.
2. **Assessment**: The bank assesses FOIR (Fixed Obligation to Income Ratio) and CIBIL to determine the loan amount.
3. **Valuation**: Independent valuers verify the property's technical and legal status.
4. **Sanction**: An in-principle approval (Sanction Letter) is issued.
5. **Disbursement**: Loan is disbursed to the seller/builder after the borrower pays their margin money.

---

## Prepayment and Foreclosure

### EMI repayment

Repayments are calculated on a monthly reducing balance basis. Customers must maintain sufficient funds in their linked <!-- BANK-SPECIFIC: Savings Account --> to ensure successful Auto-Debit/NACH clearance.

### Prepayment

Borrowers may make part-prepayments to reduce the principal balance. Refer to the [Home Loan Prepayment](../../scenarios/home-loan-prepayment.md) scenario for instructions.

### Foreclosure

Full loan closure is permitted at any time. For fixed-rate loans, please refer to the [Loan Charges](../charges/loan-charges.md) for applicable closure fees.

---

## Default

Failure to maintain sufficient balance for EMI clearance will result in a mandate bounce charge and penal interest as specified in [Loan Charges](../charges/loan-charges.md). Continued defaults will negatively impact the borrower's CIBIL score and may lead to SARFAESI proceedings against the collateral.

---

## Closure and NOC

Upon full repayment of the loan, the bank will issue a No Objection Certificate (NOC) and release the original property documents within <!-- BANK-SPECIFIC: 15 to 30 days -->. Ensure your mailing address is updated to receive the NOC.

---

## Tax Benefits

Borrowers can claim significant tax deductions under the Income Tax Act:
- **Section 80C**: Deduction up to ₹1.5 Lakhs on principal repayment.
- **Section 24(b)**: Deduction up to ₹2 Lakhs on interest payments for a self-occupied property.

| Tax Section | Benefit | Maximum Deduction |
|---|---|---|
| Section 80C | Principal Repayment | ₹1.5 Lakhs |
| Section 24(b) | Interest Payment | ₹2 Lakhs |
