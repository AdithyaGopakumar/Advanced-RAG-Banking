# Privacy Policy

## Overview
The bank is deeply committed to safeguarding customer data. This Privacy Policy outlines how the bank collects, uses, shares, and protects personal and sensitive financial information in the course of providing banking services.

---

## Regulatory Basis
- **Authority:** Government of India / Reserve Bank of India.
- **Statutory Framework:** Information Technology Act, 2000; Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP).
- **Banking Secrecy:** Governed by common law principles of banker-customer confidentiality.

---

## Scope
This policy applies to all current, former, and prospective customers who interact with the bank through its branches, website, mobile apps, or third-party service providers.

---

## Key Provisions

### 1. Data Collection and Usage
The bank collects personal information (name, contact, biometrics) and financial information (transactions, credit history). This data is strictly used for:
- Identity verification and KYC compliance.
- Processing transactions and providing core banking services.
- Fraud prevention and AML transaction monitoring.
- Credit risk assessment.

### 2. Data Sharing and Disclosure
The bank **does not sell** customer data to third parties for marketing. Data is only shared under the following conditions:
- **Statutory Requirement:** With regulatory authorities (RBI, FIU-IND), tax authorities, or law enforcement agencies under a legal warrant.
- **Credit Reporting:** With authorized Credit Information Companies (e.g., CIBIL) to maintain credit discipline.
- **Service Providers:** With vetted third-party vendors (e.g., card issuers, cloud providers) strictly under Non-Disclosure Agreements (NDAs) for operational purposes.

### 3. Data Retention
The bank retains customer data only as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined above, or as mandated by law. 
- *Example:* Under the Prevention of Money Laundering Act (PMLA), transaction records and KYC documents must be retained for at least **5 years** after the business relationship ends or the account is closed.

---

## Customer Rights
- **Right to Access:** Customers can request copies of the personal data the bank holds about them.
- **Right to Correction:** Customers can update inaccurate personal information (e.g., [Address Update](../services/address-update.md)).
- **Right to Withdraw Consent:** Customers may withdraw consent for data processing, though this may prevent the bank from providing certain services and lead to account closure.

---

## Customer Responsibilities
- Keep passwords, PINs, and OTPs strictly confidential.
- Inform the bank immediately if they suspect their personal data has been compromised.
