# Account Closure Policy

## Overview
This policy governs the circumstances and procedures under which a customer's bank account may be closed, either at the customer's request or at the bank's discretion.

---

## Regulatory Basis
- **Authority:** Reserve Bank of India (RBI).
- **Primary Source:** RBI Master Circular on Customer Service in Banks.

---

## Scope
This policy applies to all deposit accounts (Savings, Current, Term Deposits) held by individuals, entities, and trusts.

---

## Key Provisions

### 1. Customer-Initiated Closure
Customers may close their accounts at any time subject to the settlement of all outstanding dues.
- **Charges:** 
  - Closure within 14 days of opening: No charge.
  - Closure after 14 days but within 1 year: Closure charges apply (see [Account Charges](../charges/account-charges.md)).
  - Closure after 1 year: No charge.
- **Prerequisites:** All linked mandates, standing instructions, and auto-debits must be cancelled. Unused cheque leaves and debit cards must be destroyed or surrendered.

### 2. Bank-Initiated Closure
The bank reserves the right to close an account with a prior written notice of **30 days** in the following scenarios:
- **Non-Compliance with KYC:** If the customer refuses to provide Re-KYC documents.
- **Suspicious Activity:** If the account is being used for fraudulent activities or violates AML guidelines.
- **Abusive Behavior:** If the customer behaves abusively or threatens bank staff.
- **Zero Balance:** If the account maintains a zero balance for an extended period (typically > 1 year) with no customer-induced transactions, though the bank will notify the customer before closure.

### 3. Settlement of Balance
Upon closure, the remaining balance will be transferred to another bank account of the customer (via NEFT/RTGS) or issued as a Demand Draft. Cash payouts are strictly limited to RBI-mandated thresholds.

---

## Customer Rights
- Customers must receive a 30-day notice with valid reasons if the bank initiates the closure.
- The bank cannot refuse to close an account merely because the customer has not surrendered their unused chequebook (a written declaration suffices).

---

## Customer Responsibilities
- Ensure alternative arrangements are made for EMIs, SIPs, and salary credits before initiating closure.
- Settle any negative balances or pending service charges.
