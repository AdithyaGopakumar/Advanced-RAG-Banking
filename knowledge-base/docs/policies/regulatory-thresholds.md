# Regulatory Thresholds and Limits

## Overview

> [!WARNING]
> Regulatory limits, tax thresholds, and insurance coverage amounts change based on RBI guidelines and the Union Budget. According to the bank's current published regulatory guide, the following information applies.

This document serves as the Single Source of Truth (SSOT) for external regulatory thresholds that impact banking products.

---

## Current Thresholds

### 1. Deposit Insurance (DICGC)

- **Coverage Limit**: ₹5,00,000 (Rupees Five Lakhs) per depositor per bank.
- **Scope**: Includes principal and interest across all branches for savings, current, recurring, and fixed deposits.

### 2. Tax Deducted at Source (TDS) Limits

- **General Limit**: ₹40,000 interest earned per financial year.
- **Senior Citizens Limit**: ₹50,000 interest earned per financial year (under section 80TTB).
- **TDS Rate**: 10% (if PAN is provided), 20% (if PAN is not provided).

### 3. Transaction Limits

- **NEFT**: No maximum limit (subject to customer profile limits).
- **RTGS**: Minimum ₹2,00,000; no maximum limit.
- **IMPS**: Maximum ₹5,00,000 per transaction (as per latest NPCI guidelines).
- **UPI**: Maximum ₹1,00,000 per transaction (up to ₹5,00,000 for specific categories like IPO/Retail Direct Scheme).
