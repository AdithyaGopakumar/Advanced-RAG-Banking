# Loan Recovery Policy

## Overview
The Loan Recovery Policy outlines the bank's framework for recovering dues from defaulting borrowers. The policy is built on dignity, respect, and fair treatment, ensuring that recovery agents and bank staff do not resort to intimidation or harassment.

---

## Regulatory Basis
- **Authority:** Reserve Bank of India (RBI).
- **Primary Source:** RBI Guidelines on Fair Practices Code for Lenders & Guidelines on Recovery Agents.

---

## Scope
This policy applies to all retail and corporate loans, credit cards, and overdrafts where the borrower has defaulted on repayment.

---

## Key Provisions

### 1. Communication and Contact
- **Notice Period:** Borrowers will be given a formal written notice before the bank initiates serious recovery actions or repossession of collateral.
- **Calling Hours:** Recovery agents and bank staff will only contact borrowers between **08:00 AM and 07:00 PM**, unless the peculiar nature of the borrower's occupation requires otherwise.
- **Privacy:** The bank will strictly maintain the borrower's privacy and will not disclose the default to neighbors, relatives, or employers.

### 2. Code of Conduct for Recovery Agents
- All recovery agents must carry an ID card and an authorization letter from the bank.
- Agents are strictly prohibited from using abusive language, physical force, or threatening behavior.
- Any complaint against a recovery agent will be investigated promptly, and the agent will be removed from the case during the investigation.

### 3. Repossession and Sale of Collateral (Secured Loans)
- The bank aims to resolve defaults through negotiation. Repossession of assets (like a car or house) is a last resort.
- If repossession occurs, it will strictly follow the legal framework (e.g., SARFAESI Act).
- The borrower will be given a final chance to clear dues before the asset is auctioned. Any surplus from the sale, after covering the dues and costs, will be returned to the borrower.

---

## Customer Rights
- The right to receive a copy of the loan agreement and the recovery policy.
- The right to raise a formal grievance against unethical recovery practices.
- The right to be given a fair valuation of repossessed property before it is auctioned.

---

## Customer Responsibilities
- Inform the bank immediately of any financial distress to explore restructuring options.
- Keep the bank updated on any changes to communication address or contact numbers.
