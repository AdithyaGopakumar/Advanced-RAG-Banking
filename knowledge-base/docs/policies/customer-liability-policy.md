# Customer Liability Policy

## Overview
This policy defines the limits of a customer's liability in the event of an unauthorized electronic banking transaction (e.g., fraud, hacking, or card theft). It is designed to protect customers from financial loss while clearly outlining their responsibility to report incidents promptly.

---

## Regulatory Basis
- **Authority:** Reserve Bank of India (RBI).
- **Primary Source:** RBI Circular on Customer Protection – Limiting Liability of Customers in Unauthorised Electronic Banking Transactions.

---

## Scope
This policy applies to all electronic banking transactions, including ATM withdrawals, Point of Sale (PoS) transactions, Internet Banking, Mobile Banking, and UPI transfers.

---

## Key Provisions

### 1. Zero Liability of the Customer
A customer has **zero liability** (the bank bears the full loss) in the following scenarios:
- **Contributory Fraud/Negligence/Deficiency on the part of the bank:** (e.g., a security breach at the bank's end), irrespective of whether or when the customer reports it.
- **Third-Party Breach:** Where the fault lies neither with the bank nor with the customer, but lies elsewhere in the system, and the customer notifies the bank within **3 working days** of receiving the transaction communication.

### 2. Limited Liability of the Customer
If a third-party breach occurs and the customer reports it to the bank with a delay (between **4 and 7 working days**), the customer's liability is capped. 
- The maximum liability varies by account type (e.g., `<!-- BANK-SPECIFIC: ₹10,000 for standard savings accounts -->`, `<!-- BANK-SPECIFIC: ₹25,000 for premium accounts/credit cards -->`), as defined by RBI guidelines.

### 3. Full Liability of the Customer
- If the loss is due to the **customer's negligence** (e.g., sharing the payment credentials, PIN, OTP, or CVV), the customer will bear the entire loss until the moment the unauthorized transaction is reported to the bank.
- Any loss occurring *after* the reporting of the incident will be borne by the bank.

### 4. Delayed Reporting (Beyond 7 Days)
If the fraud is reported after 7 working days, the customer's liability will be determined according to the bank's Board-approved internal policy, which may result in full liability depending on the circumstances.

---

## Customer Responsibilities
- **Report Immediately:** The crucial factor in limiting liability is time. Customers must report any unauthorized transaction to the 24x7 call center or block their card via the mobile app immediately.
- **Do Not Share Credentials:** Never share PINs, OTPs, or NetBanking passwords with anyone, including bank staff.
- **Update Contact Info:** Customers must keep their mobile number and email ID updated to ensure they receive instant transaction alerts.

---

## Reversal Timelines
Upon being notified by the customer, the bank must credit (shadow reverse) the amount involved in the unauthorized transaction to the customer's account within **10 working days**.
