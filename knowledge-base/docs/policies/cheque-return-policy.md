# Cheque Return Policy

## Overview
The Cheque Return Policy outlines the bank's procedure for handling dishonoured (bounced) cheques, the applicable charges, and the actions taken against frequent defaulters to maintain the sanctity of the payment system.

---

## Regulatory Basis
- **Authority:** Reserve Bank of India (RBI) & Government of India.
- **Primary Source:** Negotiable Instruments Act, 1881 (specifically Section 138) and RBI Master Circular on Customer Service.

---

## Scope
This policy covers both inward clearing cheques (cheques issued by the customer and presented for payment) and outward clearing cheques (cheques deposited by the customer).

---

## Key Provisions

### 1. Reasons for Return
A cheque may be returned unpaid for several reasons, including:
- Insufficient Funds (Financial reason).
- Signature mismatch, stale cheque (older than 3 months), overwriting, or post-dated (Technical reasons).

### 2. Cheque Return Charges
- **Financial Returns:** If a cheque bounces due to insufficient funds, the issuer is charged a "Cheque Return Fee." In many cases, the depositor's bank may also levy a nominal fee to cover processing costs. (See [Account Charges](../charges/account-charges.md)).
- **Technical Returns:** If a cheque is returned for technical reasons (e.g., bank's sorting error), no charges are levied on the customer.

### 3. Action Against Frequent Defaulters
As per RBI guidelines, if a customer repeatedly issues cheques that bounce due to insufficient funds, the bank will take restrictive action:
- **Warning:** A warning letter is issued.
- **Facility Withdrawal:** If a customer has `<!-- BANK-SPECIFIC: 4 -->` or more cheque returns on a specific account within a financial year, the bank may withdraw the cheque book facility or close the account.

### 4. Cheque Truncation System (CTS)
Cheques are processed digitally via CTS. Physical cheques are not returned to the depositor upon bouncing; instead, a "Cheque Return Memo" is provided, which serves as legal proof of dishonour.

---

## Customer Rights
- Customers have the right to receive the Cheque Return Memo within 24 hours of the clearing cycle.
- The payee has the right to initiate legal action against the drawer under Section 138 of the Negotiable Instruments Act if a cheque bounces due to insufficient funds.

---

## Customer Responsibilities
- Ensure sufficient balance in the account *before* issuing a cheque.
- Ensure the cheque is dated correctly, amounts match in words and figures, and there are no unauthorized overwritings.
