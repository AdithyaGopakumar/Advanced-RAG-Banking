# KYC Policy

## Overview
The "Know Your Customer" (KYC) policy ensures that the bank accurately identifies and verifies the identity of its customers. This prevents the bank from being used, intentionally or unintentionally, by criminal elements for money laundering or terrorist financing activities.

---

## Regulatory Basis
- **Authority:** Reserve Bank of India (RBI).
- **Primary Source:** Master Direction - Know Your Customer (KYC) Direction, 2016 (as updated).
- **Statutory Framework:** Prevention of Money Laundering Act, 2002 (PMLA) and the PML Rules, 2005.

---

## Scope
This policy applies to all customers (individuals, companies, trusts, etc.) engaging in financial transactions with the bank, whether opening an account, depositing cash above thresholds, or conducting cross-border remittances.

---

## Key Provisions

### 1. Customer Identification Program (CIP)
The bank requires robust identification of the customer and the beneficial owner. 
- Individual customers must provide **Officially Valid Documents (OVDs)** (e.g., Aadhaar, Passport, Driving License, Voter ID).
- PAN or Form 60 is mandatory for most account openings and high-value transactions as per Income Tax Rule 114B.

### 2. Risk Management and Customer Due Diligence (CDD)
Customers are profiled into **Low, Medium, or High Risk** categories based on factors such as their nature of business, location, and transaction patterns. 
- *High-Risk* customers are subjected to Enhanced Due Diligence (EDD), including verification of the source of wealth.
- *Note:* The specific parameters and scoring algorithms used to categorize risk are internal to the bank and are not disclosed publicly.

### 3. Periodic Updation of KYC (Re-KYC)
Customers must periodically refresh their KYC documents. As per RBI guidelines, the frequency is based on the risk profile:
- **High-Risk:** Every 2 years.
- **Medium-Risk:** Every 8 years.
- **Low-Risk:** Every 10 years.

Failure to complete Re-KYC leads to gradual freezing of the account (Debit freeze followed by Total freeze).

---

## Customer Rights
- Customers have the right to use non-Aadhaar OVDs if they do not wish to authenticate via UIDAI (except where direct benefit transfers are involved).
- Customers have the right to a seamless digital Re-KYC process (e.g., via NetBanking) if their details have not changed.

---

## Customer Responsibilities
- Keep the bank informed of any changes to their communication or permanent address within 30 days.
- Respond promptly to the bank's requests for Re-KYC or transaction clarifications to prevent account freezes.
