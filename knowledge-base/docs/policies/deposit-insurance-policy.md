# Deposit Insurance Policy

## Overview
The bank is registered with the Deposit Insurance and Credit Guarantee Corporation (DICGC), a wholly-owned subsidiary of the Reserve Bank of India. This provides a safety net for depositors, ensuring that their hard-earned money is protected up to a specified statutory limit in the highly unlikely event of a bank liquidation or failure.

---

## Regulatory Basis
- **Authority:** Deposit Insurance and Credit Guarantee Corporation (DICGC).
- **Primary Source:** DICGC Act, 1961.

---

## Scope
This policy covers all eligible deposits held by individuals and entities with the bank.

---

## Key Provisions

### 1. What is Covered?
DICGC insures all bank deposits, such as:
- Savings Accounts
- Current Accounts
- Fixed Deposits (FDs)
- Recurring Deposits (RDs)

### 2. What is Not Covered?
- Deposits of foreign governments.
- Deposits of Central/State Governments.
- Inter-bank deposits.
- Any amount due on account of and deposit received outside India.

### 3. Maximum Coverage Limit
Each depositor in a bank is insured up to a maximum of `<!-- BANK-SPECIFIC: ₹5,00,000 (Rupees Five Lakhs) -->` for both principal and interest amount held in the same capacity and same right.

### 4. How the Limit is Calculated
- **Across Branches:** The limit is applied to the aggregate of all deposits held by the customer across all branches of the *same* bank.
- **Different Capacities:** If a customer holds funds in different capacities (e.g., one account as an individual, another as a partner of a firm, or as a guardian of a minor), each capacity is insured separately up to the maximum limit.

---

## Customer Rights
- Customers have the right to claim the insured amount within a stipulated time frame (usually within 90 days) if the RBI places the bank under severe restrictions or liquidation.
- The insurance premium is paid entirely by the bank; no cost is passed on to the customer.

---

## Customer Responsibilities
- Keep contact and bank details updated to ensure smooth processing in case a claim arises.
- Understand that any aggregate deposit (including interest) above the statutory limit is not covered by DICGC insurance.
