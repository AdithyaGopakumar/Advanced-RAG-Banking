# Dormant Account Policy

## Overview
To protect customers from fraud and unauthorized access, the bank proactively monitors inactive accounts. Accounts with no customer-induced transactions for an extended period are classified as Inactive and subsequently Dormant. 

---

## Regulatory Basis
- **Authority:** Reserve Bank of India (RBI).
- **Primary Source:** Master Circular on Customer Service in Banks & Master Direction on DEA Fund Scheme.

---

## Scope
This policy applies strictly to Savings and Current accounts. It does not apply to Fixed Deposits or Loan accounts.

---

## Key Provisions

### 1. Classification Timelines
- **Inactive Account:** An account becomes 'Inactive' if there are no customer-induced transactions for **12 months**.
- **Dormant / Inoperative Account:** An account becomes 'Dormant' if there are no customer-induced transactions for **24 months**.

*Note:* Dividend credits, interest credits (by the bank), or automatic penal charge deductions are NOT considered customer-induced transactions.

### 2. Restrictions on Dormant Accounts
Once an account is classified as Dormant:
- Debit transactions (ATM, UPI, NetBanking, Cheques) are blocked to prevent fraud.
- Credit transactions (inward NEFT, salary) may also be restricted depending on the bank's internal risk policy.
- **No Charges:** The bank will **not** levy any minimum balance non-maintenance charges on inoperative/dormant accounts.

### 3. Transfer to DEA Fund
If a deposit remains unclaimed for **10 years** or more, the balance is transferred to the RBI's Depositor Education and Awareness (DEA) Fund. The customer still retains the right to claim these funds from the bank at any time.

---

## Customer Rights
- The bank will notify the customer via SMS/Email at least 3 months before an account turns dormant.
- The bank does not charge any fee for the reactivation of a dormant account.
- Customers can claim their funds back even after they have been transferred to the DEA fund.

---

## Customer Responsibilities
- Conduct at least one debit or credit transaction annually to keep the account active.
- To reactivate a dormant account, the customer must submit a reactivation request along with fresh KYC documents.
