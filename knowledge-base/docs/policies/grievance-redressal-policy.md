# Grievance Redressal Policy

## Overview
The bank is committed to providing prompt and effective resolution to customer complaints. This policy outlines the framework for recording, escalating, and resolving customer grievances, ensuring that customers are treated fairly and their issues are addressed transparently.

---

## Regulatory Basis
- **Authority:** Reserve Bank of India (RBI).
- **Primary Source:** Reserve Bank of India - Integrated Ombudsman Scheme, 2021 (RB-IOS).
- **Statutory Framework:** Master Circular on Customer Service in Banks.

---

## Scope
This policy covers all customer complaints related to the bank's products, services, staff behavior, and digital channels. It does not cover commercial decisions of the bank, such as credit risk assessments for loan approvals.

---

## Key Provisions

### 1. Multi-Tier Resolution Matrix
The bank operates a strict 3-tier internal escalation matrix:
- **Level 1 (Branch / Call Center):** The first point of contact. All complaints must be logged and assigned a Service Request Number (SRN).
- **Level 2 (Nodal Officer):** If unresolved within 10 days, the complaint is escalated to the regional Grievance Redressal Officer.
- **Level 3 (Principal Nodal Officer):** The highest internal authority. Must provide a final resolution within 5 days of escalation.

### 2. Time-Bound Resolution
- The bank mandates that all internal complaints (across all 3 levels combined) must be resolved or formally replied to within a maximum of **30 days** from the date of initial receipt.
- Failed ATM/UPI transactions have specific automated reversal TATs (e.g., T+5 days) mandated by RBI. Delays beyond this attract a daily penalty payable to the customer.

### 3. External Escalation
If the bank rejects the complaint or fails to reply within 30 days, the customer will be clearly informed of their right to escalate the matter to the RBI Ombudsman.

---

## Customer Rights
- Customers have the right to receive an acknowledgment (with an SRN) for every complaint filed.
- Customers have the right to escalate their complaint internally without facing prejudice.
- Customers have the right to approach the RBI Ombudsman free of charge if the bank fails to provide a satisfactory resolution.

---

## Customer Responsibilities
- Keep the Service Request Number (SRN) handy for all follow-ups.
- Do not jump the escalation matrix. Always start at Level 1, as higher authorities will require the base SRN to track the history of the issue.
