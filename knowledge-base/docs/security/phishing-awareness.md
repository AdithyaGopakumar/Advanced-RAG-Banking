# Phishing Awareness

## Overview
Phishing is a cybercrime where targets are contacted by email, telephone (Vishing), or text message (Smishing) by someone posing as a legitimate institution (like the bank or RBI) to lure individuals into providing sensitive data such as personally identifiable information, banking and credit card details, and passwords.

---

## Key Guidelines
- **Sender Verification:** Always check the sender's email address carefully. A legitimate bank email will end in `@bank.com` (e.g., `alerts@bank.com`), not `@gmail.com` or `@bank-support-update.com`.
- **Link Inspection:** Hover over links in emails before clicking them. Ensure the URL begins with `https://` and matches the bank's exact domain.
- **Urgency is a Red Flag:** Phishing attempts often use fear tactics (e.g., "Your account will be suspended in 24 hours if you do not update your KYC here"). The bank will never send such threatening links.

---

## Dos and Don'ts

### Dos
- **Do** type the bank's URL directly into your browser rather than clicking a link in an email.
- **Do** look for the padlock icon in the browser address bar when entering passwords.
- **Do** forward suspicious emails claiming to be from the bank to `<!-- BANK-SPECIFIC: report.phishing@bank.com -->`.

### Don'ts
- **Don't** open attachments from unknown or suspicious email addresses, as they may contain malware or keyloggers.
- **Don't** reply to suspicious SMS messages or call the numbers provided in them.
- **Don't** trust caller ID blindly; caller IDs can be easily spoofed to look like the bank's official number.

---

## How to Report
If you have accidentally clicked a phishing link or shared your details:
1. **Change Passwords:** Immediately log in (by typing the URL manually) and change your Internet Banking password and UPI PIN.
2. **Block Accounts:** Use the mobile app to block all cards.
3. **Notify the Bank:** Call the 24x7 helpline `<!-- BANK-SPECIFIC: 1800-XXX-XXXX -->` to report a potential compromise.
