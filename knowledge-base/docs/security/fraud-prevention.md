# Fraud Prevention

## Overview
Fraud prevention is a shared responsibility between the bank and the customer. The bank employs advanced monitoring systems to detect suspicious activities, but customer awareness is the strongest defense against social engineering and unauthorized access.

---

## Key Guidelines
- **Zero Trust:** Never trust unsolicited calls, emails, or SMS messages claiming to be from the bank, especially if they create a sense of urgency (e.g., "Your account will be blocked today").
- **Official Channels Only:** Always use the official banking app downloaded from the Google Play Store or Apple App Store. Never download APK files sent via WhatsApp or email.
- **Transaction Alerts:** Always ensure that your current mobile number is registered with the bank to receive instant SMS and email alerts for every transaction.

---

## Dos and Don'ts

### Dos
- **Do** review your bank statements and SMS alerts regularly.
- **Do** lock your smartphone with a strong password or biometric authentication.
- **Do** report a lost phone or lost SIM card to your telecom provider and the bank immediately.
- **Do** use the in-app feature to disable international and online transactions when not in use.

### Don'ts
- **Don't** share your OTP (One Time Password), PIN, CVV, or Internet Banking password with *anyone*, including individuals claiming to be bank employees or RBI officials.
- **Don't** install screen-sharing apps (like AnyDesk or TeamViewer) if instructed by an unknown caller.
- **Don't** click on links in SMS messages promising rewards, KYC updates, or free gifts.
- **Don't** use public Wi-Fi networks to conduct financial transactions.

---

## How to Report Fraud
If you suspect that your account has been compromised, you must act immediately to minimize your liability under the [Customer Liability Policy](../policies/customer-liability-policy.md):
1. **Block the Channel:** Instantly block your Debit/Credit card or freeze your UPI ID using the Mobile Banking app.
2. **Call Customer Care:** Dial the 24x7 toll-free helpline at `<!-- BANK-SPECIFIC: 1800-XXX-XXXX -->` to report the unauthorized transaction.
3. **Cyber Crime Portal:** Register a formal complaint on the National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal (https://cybercrime.gov.in) or dial 1930.
