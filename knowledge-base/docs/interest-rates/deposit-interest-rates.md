## Senior Citizen Rates
Senior citizens (aged 60 years and above) are eligible for an additional premium over the regular fixed deposit rates.

- **Premium Rate:** <!-- BANK-SPECIFIC: e.g., 0.50% --> additional interest per annum across all tenures.
- **Eligibility:** The primary account holder must be a senior citizen on the date of deposit opening.
- **Applicability:** Available for resident Indian individuals only (not applicable for NRE/NRO accounts).
