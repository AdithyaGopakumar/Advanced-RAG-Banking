# Card Interest Rates

This document serves as the Single Source of Truth (SSOT) for all interest rates and finance charges applicable to Credit Cards.

## 1. Retail Purchases (Revolving Credit)

Finance charges are applied when the Total Amount Due (TAD) is not paid in full by the Payment Due Date. Interest is calculated on a daily reducing balance method from the date of the transaction.

| Card Category | Monthly Interest Rate | Annualized Percentage Rate (APR) |
|---|---|---|
| Premium & Super Premium Cards | <!-- BANK-SPECIFIC: 2.99% --> | <!-- BANK-SPECIFIC: 35.88% --> |
| Standard & Co-branded Cards | <!-- BANK-SPECIFIC: 3.49% --> | <!-- BANK-SPECIFIC: 41.88% --> |
| Secured Credit Cards (Against FD) | <!-- BANK-SPECIFIC: 1.99% --> | <!-- BANK-SPECIFIC: 23.88% --> |

## 2. Cash Advances

Cash advances do not have an interest-free grace period. Interest is charged from the date of withdrawal until the date of full settlement.

## 3. Grace Period (Interest-Free Period)

Credit cards offer an interest-free grace period on retail purchases provided the previous month's outstanding balance was cleared in full.
- **Minimum Grace Period:** <!-- BANK-SPECIFIC: 18 days -->
- **Maximum Grace Period:** <!-- BANK-SPECIFIC: 48 days -->

*Note: The grace period does not apply to cash advances, balance transfers, or if there is an existing revolving balance on the card.*
