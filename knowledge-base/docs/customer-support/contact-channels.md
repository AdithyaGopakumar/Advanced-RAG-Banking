# Contact Channels

## Overview

The bank offers multiple channels for customers to seek assistance, report fraud, or register complaints. For urgent issues like a lost debit card or suspected fraud, always use the 24x7 toll-free helpline.

---

- **Domestic Toll-Free:** `<!-- BANK-SPECIFIC: 1800-XXX-XXXX -->`
- **NRI Helpdesk:** `<!-- BANK-SPECIFIC: +91-XX-XXXX-XXXX -->` (International call charges apply).
- **Available For:** Blocking cards, reporting unauthorized transactions, checking balances, and registering complaints.
- *Note:* The bank will never ask for your PIN, OTP, or CVV over the phone.

## Digital Support Channels

- **Email Support:** `<!-- BANK-SPECIFIC: customercare@bank.com -->` 
  - Standard response time: 24 to 48 hours.
  - Always email from your registered email address for faster resolution.
- **WhatsApp Banking:** Send "Hi" to `<!-- BANK-SPECIFIC: +91-XXXXXXXXXX -->`.
- **Chatbot / Live Chat:** Available 24x7 on the bank's official website and mobile app.

---

## Branch Support

For complex issues, legal claims, or physical document submissions:
- **Home Branch:** The specific branch where your account was opened. Certain services (like account closure or name changes) must be done here.
- **Any Branch Banking:** For general queries, cash deposits, or updating address/KYC, you can visit any branch of the bank in India.

---

## Lost/Stolen Cards (Emergency)

If you lose your card or notice a fraudulent transaction, you must act immediately to limit your liability:
1. Block the card instantly via the Mobile Banking app.
2. SMS `BLOCK <Last 4 digits of card>` to `<!-- BANK-SPECIFIC: 56767XX -->`.
3. Call the 24x7 helpline.
