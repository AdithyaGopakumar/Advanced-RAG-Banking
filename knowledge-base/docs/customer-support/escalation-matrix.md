# Escalation Matrix

## Overview

If a customer's initial complaint (Level 1) is not resolved to their satisfaction, or if there is an unreasonable delay, they have the right to escalate the issue up the bank's management hierarchy.

---

## The Escalation Path

### Level 1: Initial Complaint
- **Who:** Branch Manager or Customer Care (Toll-Free).
- **Resolution TAT:** Normally up to 10 working days (except for specific ATM/UPI disputes governed by network rules).
- **Requirement:** You must obtain a Service Request Number (SRN).

### Level 2: Grievance Redressal Officer (Nodal Officer)
If you are dissatisfied with the Level 1 response, or if 10 days have passed:
- **How to Escalate:** Download the Grievance Form from the bank's website or write an email directly to the Nodal Officer. (Email: `<!-- BANK-SPECIFIC: nodal.officer@bank.com -->`).
- **Required Info:** You must quote your original Level 1 SRN.
- **Resolution TAT:** The Nodal Officer will respond within **5 to 7 working days**.

### Level 3: Principal Nodal Officer (PNO)
If the Nodal Officer's response is unsatisfactory:
- **How to Escalate:** Write to the Principal Nodal Officer. (Email: `<!-- BANK-SPECIFIC: pno@bank.com -->`).
- **Resolution TAT:** The PNO is the highest internal authority and will provide the bank's final stance within **5 working days**.

---

## External Escalation

If your grievance is not resolved by the Principal Nodal Officer within **30 days** of your *original* Level 1 complaint, or if the bank rejects your complaint outright, you can escalate the matter externally to the RBI.

Please refer to the [Banking Ombudsman](banking-ombudsman.md) process for external escalation.

---

## Important Rules

- Do not jump directly to Level 3. The PNO will simply route the complaint back to Level 1 if it does not have a prior SRN.
- Certain issues, like updating a mobile number or court-ordered freezes, cannot be overridden by the PNO due to strict regulatory or legal constraints.
