# Complaint Process

## Overview

The bank is committed to resolving customer grievances efficiently and transparently. As per the RBI's Grievance Redressal framework, all customer complaints must be recorded, acknowledged, and resolved within a defined timeframe.

---

## How to Register a Complaint (Level 1)

Customers should first register their complaint at **Level 1** before escalating it.

1. **Via Internet/Mobile Banking (Recommended):**
   - Navigate to `Customer Service > Register Complaint`.
   - Select the category (e.g., ATM, UPI, Branch Service).
   - Enter details and submit. An SRN (Service Request Number) is generated instantly.
2. **Via the 24x7 Call Center:**
   - Call the toll-free customer care number (see [Contact Channels](contact-channels.md)).
   - Follow the IVR or speak to an agent to log the issue.
3. **At the Branch:**
   - Ask for the physical complaint register, or submit a written letter to the Branch Manager.
   - Always insist on a physical acknowledgment receipt with the date and the bank's seal.

---

## What Happens Next?

1. **Acknowledgement:** You will receive an SMS/Email acknowledging your complaint along with a unique tracking number (SRN).
2. **Investigation:** The relevant department investigates the issue. 
3. **Resolution:** 
   - You will receive a final response via email or letter.
   - For failed digital transactions (like ATM cash not dispensed), auto-reversals often happen within `T+1` to `T+5` days.

---

## Tracking Your Complaint

You can track the status of your complaint at any time by:
- Entering the SRN on the bank's website under the 'Track Request' section.
- Calling the customer care helpline.

---

## If You Are Not Satisfied

If your complaint is closed without a satisfactory resolution, or if you do not receive a response within **10 working days**, you should escalate the issue to **Level 2** (Nodal Officer). See the [Escalation Matrix](escalation-matrix.md).
