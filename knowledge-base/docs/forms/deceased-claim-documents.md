# Deceased Claim Documents

## Overview
Settling the accounts of a deceased customer is a sensitive priority. The documentation required differs significantly depending on whether a valid nomination was registered on the account.

---

## Claim Form (With Nomination)

- **Form Name:** Deceased Claim Form (Annexure 1 - With Nomination)
- **Form Type:** Request / Claim
- **Purpose:** To transfer the balances of a deceased account holder to the registered nominee.
- **Who Needs It:** The registered nominee.
- **When It Is Required:** Following the death of the primary account holder.
- **Information Required:** Deceased customer's details, Nominee's details, Destination account details.
- **Supporting Documents:**
  - Original Death Certificate (for OSV by the bank).
  - KYC of the Nominee (Identity and Address Proof).
  - Passbook / Unused Cheques / Debit Card of the deceased.
- **Signature/Authorization:** Signature of the Nominee.
- **Where to Obtain:** Bank Branch or `<!-- BANK-SPECIFIC: Download URL -->`.
- **Where to Submit:** Any bank branch.
- **Digital/Physical Availability:** Physical submission required for verification of the original Death Certificate.
- **Processing:** Settled within 15 days of submitting complete documents (as per RBI guidelines).
- **What Happens After Submission:** Balances are transferred via DD/NEFT to the nominee, and the deceased's account is closed.

---

## Claim Form (Without Nomination)

- **Form Name:** Deceased Claim Form (Annexure 2 - Without Nomination)
- **Form Type:** Request / Claim
- **Purpose:** To settle balances when no nominee is registered.
- **Who Needs It:** Legal Heirs of the deceased.
- **Supporting Documents:**
  - Original Death Certificate.
  - KYC of all Legal Heirs.
  - Legal Heir Certificate / Succession Certificate (depending on the claim amount).
  - **Indemnity Bond:** Stamped indemnity signed by all legal heirs.
  - **Letter of Disclaimer:** From heirs relinquishing their right in favor of one specific heir.
- **Digital/Physical Availability:** Physical only.
