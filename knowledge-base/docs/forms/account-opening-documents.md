# Account Opening Documents

## Overview
This document specifies the required documentation for opening various types of retail banking accounts. Account opening requires both Know Your Customer (KYC) documentation and product-specific forms.

---

## Required Documents

### For individual applicants (Savings, Term Deposits)

1. **Account Opening Form (AOF)**: Duly filled and signed by all applicants.
2. **KYC Documents**: Proof of Identity (POI) and Proof of Address (POA). See [KYC Documents](kyc-documents.md) for the acceptable list of Officially Valid Documents (OVDs).
3. **Recent Passport-size Photographs**: Usually 2 photographs per applicant.
4. **PAN Card**: Mandatory for account opening. If PAN is not available, Form 60 must be submitted.

### Product-Specific Additional Documents

- **Salary Account**: 
  - Employer introduction letter OR
  - Latest salary slip (not older than 3 months) OR
  - ID card issued by the employer.
- **Minor Account**: 
  - Birth certificate of the minor.
  - KYC documents of the natural or legal guardian.
- **NRI Accounts (NRE / NRO / FCNR)**:
  - Copy of valid passport (pages containing details like name, address, date of birth, date of issue, expiry, etc.).
  - Copy of valid visa or residence permit.
  - Overseas address proof.
- **Joint Account**:
  - KYC documents and photographs for all joint holders.
  - Mandate for account operation (e.g., "Either or Survivor").

### For business applicants (Current Account)

1. **Entity KYC**: Registration certificate, partnership deed, trust deed, or certificate of incorporation, depending on the entity type.
2. **Beneficial Ownership**: Declaration of beneficial owners and their KYC documents.
3. **Authorized Signatories**: Board resolution or mandate identifying authorized signatories, along with their KYC documents.
4. **PAN Card**: PAN of the entity.

---

## How to Submit

- **Branch Application**: Submit self-attested physical copies along with the original documents for verification at any bank branch.
- **Video KYC (V-KYC)**: For digital account opening, documents (PAN and Aadhaar) are captured and verified during the video call with a bank executive. Original PAN card must be shown during the call.
