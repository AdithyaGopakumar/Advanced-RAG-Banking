# Loan Application Documents

## Overview

This document is the Single Source of Truth (SSOT) for the documentary evidence required to process retail loan applications. It standardizes the proof of identity, address, income, and collateral across all lending products.

---

## Required Documents

### For individual applicants (Salaried)

| Document Type | Accepted Documents |
|---|---|
| **KYC (Identity & Address)** | Refer to [KYC Documents](kyc-documents.md) for standard valid proofs (Aadhaar, PAN, Passport, Voter ID). |
| **Income Proof** | <ul><li>Latest 3 months salary slips</li><li>Latest Form 16 / ITR</li><li>Latest 6 months bank statement (salary account)</li></ul> |
| **Employment Proof** | ID Card, Employer letter, or Employment contract. |

### For self-employed individuals and business applicants

| Document Type | Accepted Documents |
|---|---|
| **KYC (Identity & Address)** | Entity proofs (GST Certificate, Udyam Registration, Partnership Deed, Certificate of Incorporation) + Proprietor/Partner/Director KYC. |
| **Income/Financial Proof** | <ul><li>Latest 2-3 years ITR with computation of income</li><li>Audited Balance Sheet and P&L statements</li><li>Latest 6-12 months bank statement (Current Account)</li></ul> |
| **Business Vintage Proof** | Shop & Establishment act certificate, Trade license, or GST registration showing vintage. |

### Collateral Specific Documents

| Loan Type | Additional Required Documents |
|---|---|
| **Home Loan / LAP** | Sale Deed / Agreement to Sell, Chain of past titles, Approved building plan, NOC from builder/society, Property Tax receipt. |
| **Vehicle Loan** | Proforma Invoice / Quotation from authorized dealer. |
| **Gold Loan** | Not applicable (Gold items are physically appraised at branch). |
| **Loan Against FD** | Original FD Receipt (if physical) properly discharged. |

---

## How to Submit

- **Digital Applications**: Documents can be uploaded as scanned PDFs or JPEGs via Internet Banking, Mobile Banking, or the loan application portal. E-KYC via Aadhaar OTP/Biometric is accepted.
- **Branch Applications**: Self-attested photocopies must be submitted. The original documents must be produced for 'Original Seen and Verified' (OSV) by the bank official.
