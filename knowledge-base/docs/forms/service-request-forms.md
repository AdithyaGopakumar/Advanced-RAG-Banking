# Service Request Forms

## Account Closure Form

- **Form Name:** Account Closure Request Form
- **Form Type:** Request
- **Purpose:** To formally close a savings, current, or deposit account.
- **Who Needs It:** Primary and Joint Account Holders.
- **When It Is Required:** When opting to close the banking relationship.
- **Applicable Product/Service:** Savings Accounts, Current Accounts, Fixed Deposits.
- **Information Required:** Account Number, Reason for Closure, Destination Account Details (for transferring the remaining balance).
- **Supporting Documents:** Unused Cheque Leaves and Debit Cards (must be surrendered/destroyed).
- **Signature/Authorization:** Requires the wet signature of ALL joint holders as per the operating mandate.
- **Where to Obtain:** Any bank branch or downloadable from `<!-- BANK-SPECIFIC: URL -->`.
- **Where to Submit:** Home branch.
- **Digital/Physical Availability:** Physical only (for full relationship closure).
- **What Happens After Submission:** The account is zeroized and marked 'Closed'. A closure confirmation is sent via SMS.
- **Official Source:** Bank Operations Department.

## Signature Modification Form

- **Form Name:** Signature Change Request
- **Form Type:** Request
- **Purpose:** To update the specimen signature on file.
- **Who Needs It:** Customers whose signature has changed or who forgot their old signature.
- **When It Is Required:** To prevent cheque bouncing or form rejections due to signature mismatch.
- **Information Required:** Old Signature (if remembered), New Signature (signed in front of the bank official).
- **Supporting Documents:** Valid Photo ID (OVD).
- **Signature/Authorization:** Must be signed in the presence of the Branch Manager for verification.
- **Where to Obtain:** Bank branch.
- **Where to Submit:** Home branch only.
- **Digital/Physical Availability:** Physical only.
