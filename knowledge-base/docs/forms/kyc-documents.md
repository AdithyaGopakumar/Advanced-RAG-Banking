# KYC Documents

## Overview
This document lists the Officially Valid Documents (OVDs) accepted by the Bank for establishing the identity and address of customers, in accordance with the Reserve Bank of India (RBI) KYC Master Direction.

---

## Required Documents

### For individual applicants (Officially Valid Documents)

As per RBI guidelines, customers must submit any one of the following OVDs that serves as both Proof of Identity (POI) and Proof of Address (POA), provided it contains the current address:

1. **Aadhaar Card** (or proof of possession of Aadhaar number)
2. **Passport**
3. **Driving Licence**
4. **Voter's Identity Card** issued by the Election Commission of India
5. **Job Card** issued by NREGA duly signed by an officer of the State Government
6. **Letter issued by the National Population Register** containing details of name and address

*Note: PAN Card is mandatory for most financial transactions but is only a Proof of Identity, not a Proof of Address.*

### Simplified KYC for BSBDA / PMJDY

Customers opening a Basic Savings Bank Deposit Account (BSBDA) or PMJDY account who do not have the standard OVDs may be allowed to open a "Small Account" by submitting:

- A self-attested photograph
- Signature or thumb impression in the presence of a bank official

*Note: Small accounts have restricted limits on credits, withdrawals, and balances. Standard OVDs must be submitted within 12 months to convert to a regular account.*

### For business applicants

- **Sole Proprietorship**: Registration certificate, certificate/licence issued by the municipal authorities, GST certificate, etc.
- **Partnership**: Registration certificate, Partnership deed, PAN of the partnership firm.
- **Companies**: Certificate of incorporation, Memorandum and Articles of Association (MoA & AoA), PAN of the company, Board resolution.

---

## How to Submit

- **Self-Attestation**: All submitted document copies must be self-attested by the customer.
- **Original Verification**: Original documents must be presented to the bank official for "Original Seen and Verified" (OSV) validation.
- **Digital Submission**: For e-KYC or Video KYC, Aadhaar XML or secure OTP-based fetching from UIDAI is used in place of physical copies.
