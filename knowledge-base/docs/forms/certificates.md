# Certificates

## Interest Certificate

- **Form Name:** Provisional / Final Interest Certificate
- **Form Type:** Certificate
- **Purpose:** Provides a summary of the interest earned on deposits or interest paid on loans during a specific financial year.
- **Who Needs It:** Customers filing their Income Tax Returns (ITR).
- **When It Is Required:** End of the financial year (March/April).
- **Applicable Product/Service:** Savings Accounts, Fixed Deposits, Home Loans, Education Loans.
- **Where to Obtain:** 
  - **Digital:** Downloadable instantly via Internet Banking or Mobile Banking app (under 'Services' -> 'Certificates').
  - **Physical:** Can be requested at the home branch.
- **Processing:** Instant (if downloaded digitally).
- **Official Source:** Generated by the Core Banking System (CBS).

---

## TDS Certificate (Form 16A)

- **Form Name:** Form 16A
- **Form Type:** Tax Certificate (Regulatory)
- **Purpose:** Proof that Tax Deducted at Source (TDS) has been deducted and deposited with the Income Tax Department on behalf of the customer.
- **Who Needs It:** Customers whose interest income exceeds the TDS threshold limit (e.g., ₹40,000 for standard, ₹50,000 for senior citizens).
- **When It Is Required:** Issued quarterly by the bank.
- **Where to Obtain:** Available on Internet Banking or can be collected from the branch.
- **Official Source:** TRACES (TDS Reconciliation Analysis and Correction Enabling System).

---

## Loan No Objection Certificate (NOC)

- **Form Name:** Loan Closure NOC
- **Form Type:** Certificate
- **Purpose:** Official declaration from the bank that a specific loan has been fully repaid and the bank holds no further claim or lien on the collateral (Property/Vehicle).
- **When It Is Required:** Immediately after fully pre-paying or completing the final EMI of a secured loan. Required to remove hypothecation from the RTO (for vehicles) or clear the title (for property).
- **Processing:** Issued within `<!-- BANK-SPECIFIC: 15 working days -->` of loan closure.
- **Where to Obtain:** Mailed to the registered physical address.
