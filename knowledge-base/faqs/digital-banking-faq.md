# Digital Banking FAQ

## 1. Digital Login Issues
### Intent: `digital_login_troubleshooting`
**Variations**:
- I forgot my Internet Banking password, how do I reset it?
- My mobile banking app is locked, what should I do?
- How do I change my MPIN?

**Response Route**: `STATIC_RAG`
**Answer**: You can reset your Internet Banking password or Mobile App MPIN using the "Forgot Password/PIN" option on the login screen. You will need your Debit Card details or your profile password for authentication.
**Canonical Documents**: [Internet Banking Troubleshooting](../docs/digital-banking/internet-banking-troubleshooting.md) and [Mobile Banking Troubleshooting](../docs/digital-banking/mobile-banking-troubleshooting.md)
