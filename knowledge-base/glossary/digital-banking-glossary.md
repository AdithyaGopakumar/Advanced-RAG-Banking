# Digital Banking Glossary

## VPA
**Short Definition**: Your unique UPI ID (like an email address for money).

**Standard Definition**: Virtual Payment Address. A unique identifier that helps UPI map payments to a bank account without sharing the account number.

**Customer May Say**: "my UPI ID", "my UPI name"

**Synonyms**: UPI ID

**Acronyms / Abbreviations**: Virtual Payment Address

**Common Misspellings**: upi id

**Related Terms**: UPI, Payment

**Confusable Terms**: Account Number
