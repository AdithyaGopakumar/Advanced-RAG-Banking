# Loans Glossary

## Foreclosure
**Short Definition**: Paying off your entire loan before the tenure ends.

**Standard Definition**: The process of completely paying off the outstanding principal balance and associated interest before the scheduled end of the loan tenure.

**Customer May Say**: "close my loan early", "pay off loan fully"

**Synonyms**: Full Prepayment, Loan Closure

**Acronyms / Abbreviations**: None

**Common Misspellings**: forclosure, fourclosure

**Related Terms**: Prepayment Penalty, NOC

**Confusable Terms**: Part-Payment (which reduces principal but doesn't close the loan)
