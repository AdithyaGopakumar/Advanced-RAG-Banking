# Accounts Glossary

## Account Closure
**Short Definition**: The process of permanently closing a bank account.

**Standard Definition**: The formal termination of a banking relationship for a specific account, requiring settlement of dues and surrender of debit cards/cheque books.

**Customer May Say**: "shut my account", "cancel my account", "delete my account"

**Synonyms**: Terminate Account, Deactivate Account

**Acronyms / Abbreviations**: None

**Common Misspellings**: acount closure, closeing account

**Related Terms**: Dormant Account, Settlement

**Confusable Terms**: Account Dormancy (which is a temporary freeze due to inactivity)

**Canonical Documents**: [Account Closure Policy](../docs/policies/account-closure-policy.md)

**Related FAQs**: [Accounts FAQ](../faqs/accounts-faq.md)

**Related Scenarios**: None

**Related Decision Guides**: None

**Source / Authority**: Operations Manual

## Average Monthly Balance
**Short Definition**: The minimum balance you must maintain in your account on average over a month.

**Standard Definition**: The sum of end-of-day balances divided by the number of days in the month. Falling below this incurs penalty charges.

**Customer May Say**: "how much money to keep", "minimum balance penalty"

**Synonyms**: Minimum Balance, MAB

**Acronyms / Abbreviations**: AMB

**Common Misspellings**: avarage balance, min balance

**Related Terms**: Non-Maintenance Charges, Basic Savings Account

**Confusable Terms**: Ledger Balance (which is the actual balance at a given moment)
