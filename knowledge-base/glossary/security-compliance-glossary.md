# Security & Compliance Glossary

## Unauthorized Transaction
**Short Definition**: Money taken from your account without your permission.

**Standard Definition**: A debit transaction executed by a third party without the knowledge, consent, or authorization of the account holder, typically indicating fraud.

**Customer May Say**: "someone stole my money", "I got hacked", "fake transaction"

**Synonyms**: Fraudulent Transaction, Scam

**Acronyms / Abbreviations**: None

**Common Misspellings**: unauthorised, fraudulant

**Related Terms**: Phishing, Zero Liability Policy, Shadow Credit

**Confusable Terms**: Disputed Transaction (which involves a willing purchase but non-delivery of goods)
