# Deposits Glossary

## Premature Closure
**Short Definition**: Breaking your Fixed Deposit before its maturity date.

**Standard Definition**: The act of withdrawing funds from a term deposit before the completion of the contracted tenure, usually attracting a penalty of 0.5% to 1%.

**Customer May Say**: "break my FD", "take money out of FD early"

**Synonyms**: Break Deposit, Early Withdrawal

**Acronyms / Abbreviations**: None

**Common Misspellings**: pre-mature closure

**Related Terms**: Penalty, Tax Saver FD

**Confusable Terms**: Maturity (which is withdrawing at the agreed date)
