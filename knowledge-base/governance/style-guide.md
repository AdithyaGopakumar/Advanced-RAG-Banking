# Style Guide

This document defines the writing standards for all content in the Banking Customer Support Knowledge Base.

---

## Audience

All documentation is written for **bank customers**. Assume the reader:

- May not have a financial background
- Is looking for a clear, direct answer
- May be reading on a mobile device
- May be anxious or frustrated (especially for complaints, disputes, and security issues)
- May have the content read to them by an AI assistant

---

## Tone and Voice

### Do

- Be **clear and direct** — say what you mean in as few words as possible
- Be **helpful and supportive** — guide the customer toward a solution
- Be **respectful** — use polite, professional language
- Be **neutral** — present facts without persuasion or marketing
- Be **confident** — use definitive statements when the information is certain

### Do Not

- Do not use **marketing language** ("Best-in-class!", "Exciting offer!", "Don't miss out!")
- Do not use **jargon** without explanation
- Do not use **condescending language** ("Simply do X", "It's easy, just...")
- Do not use **hedging language** when the information is definite ("You might need to..." when they definitely do)
- Do not use **passive voice** unless it improves clarity

---

## Language Standards

### General Rules

| Rule | Example (Correct) | Example (Incorrect) |
|---|---|---|
| Use active voice | "The Bank credits interest monthly." | "Interest is credited monthly by the Bank." |
| Use second person | "You can transfer funds using NEFT." | "The customer can transfer funds using NEFT." |
| Use present tense | "This process takes 2 working days." | "This process will take 2 working days." |
| Use British English spellings | "organisation", "centre", "cheque" | "organization", "center", "check" |
| Spell out numbers under 10 | "You need three documents." | "You need 3 documents." |
| Use numerals for 10 and above | "The limit is 25 transactions." | "The limit is twenty-five transactions." |
| Use numerals for amounts | "₹10,000", "₹1,00,000" | "ten thousand rupees" |
| Use the Indian numbering system | "₹1,00,000" (1 lakh) | "₹100,000" |

### Currency

- Always use the Indian Rupee symbol: **₹**
- Format: `₹X,XX,XXX` (Indian numbering system with commas)
- Examples: ₹500, ₹10,000, ₹1,00,000, ₹50,00,000

### Dates

- Format: **DD Month YYYY** (e.g., "02 August 2026")
- In tables or metadata: **YYYY-MM-DD** (e.g., "2026-08-02")
- Never use ambiguous formats like "02/08/2026"

### Time

- Use **12-hour format** with AM/PM for customer-facing content
- Use **24-hour format** in technical or operational contexts
- Always specify the timezone: **IST (Indian Standard Time)**
- Example: "10:00 AM IST" or "between 9:00 AM and 5:00 PM IST"

## Sentence and Paragraph Style

### Sentences

- Keep sentences **short** — aim for 15–25 words
- One idea per sentence
- Lead with the most important information
- Avoid nested clauses

### Paragraphs

- Keep paragraphs to **2–4 sentences**
- One topic per paragraph
- Use a blank line between paragraphs
- Start with a topic sentence

### Examples

**Good:**

> To open a Savings Account, visit your nearest branch with a valid ID proof and OVD (Officially Valid Document). The account is activated within 1 working day.

**Bad:**

> If you would like to open a Savings Account with the Bank, which is one of our most popular products and has been serving customers for many years, you can visit any of our branches located across the country, where our staff will be happy to assist you with the process of opening an account, provided you bring the necessary documents.

---

## Lists

### When to Use Lists

- Use **bullet lists** for items with no specific order
- Use **numbered lists** for sequential steps or ranked items
- Use lists when there are **three or more items**
- Do not use a list for a single item or two items that work better as a sentence

### List Formatting

- Start each item with a **capital letter**
- End each item with **no punctuation** (unless it is a complete sentence)
- Keep list items **parallel in structure**
- Do not mix sentence fragments and complete sentences in the same list

### Examples

**Good (parallel structure):**

- Valid PAN card
- Aadhaar card or Voter ID
- Recent passport-size photograph
- OVD (Officially Valid Document) (utility bill or bank statement)

**Bad (inconsistent structure):**

- PAN card is needed
- Aadhaar card
- You should bring a photograph
- An OVD (Officially Valid Document) document such as a utility bill

## Tables

- Use tables for **structured comparisons** and **reference data**
- Include a **header row** with descriptive column names
- Align content consistently
- Keep cell content brief — use links for detailed information
- Use "N/A" or "Not applicable" for empty cells (never leave cells blank)

---

## Abbreviations and Acronyms

- **Spell out on first use** in every document, followed by the abbreviation in parentheses
- After the first use, the abbreviation alone is acceptable
- Common banking abbreviations that customers are expected to know (ATM, PIN, EMI) do not need to be spelled out

### Examples

- "Know Your Customer (KYC)" → then "KYC" for subsequent uses
- "National Electronic Funds Transfer (NEFT)" → then "NEFT"
- "ATM" — no need to spell out

### Standard Abbreviations (No Spell-Out Needed)

| Abbreviation | Meaning |
|---|---|
| ATM | Automated Teller Machine |
| PIN | Personal Identification Number |
| EMI | Equated Monthly Instalment |
| OTP | One-Time Password |
| UPI | Unified Payments Interface |
| PAN | Permanent Account Number |

For a complete list, see the [Terminology Guidelines](terminology-guidelines.md).

## Referring to the Bank

- Use **"the Bank"** (capitalised) when referring to the institution
- Do not use "we", "our", or "us"
- Do not use a fictional or real bank name

### Examples

- **Good**: "The Bank offers a Savings Account with no minimum balance requirement."
- **Bad**: "We offer a Savings Account with no minimum balance requirement."
- **Bad**: "National Trust Bank offers a Savings Account..."

---

## Product and Service Names

- **Capitalise** official product names: "Savings Account", "Fixed Deposit", "Home Loan"
- Do not capitalise generic banking terms: "interest rate", "account balance", "transaction"
- Be consistent — once a term is used, never use a different name for the same thing

---

## Regulatory References

- Refer to the Reserve Bank of India as **"RBI"** (spell out on first use per document)
- When citing regulations, include the circular or guideline reference where possible
- Example: "As per RBI guidelines (RBI/2022-23/123), customers must complete KYC within 30 days."

---

## Sensitive Topics

When writing about:

- **Fraud or security breaches** — Be factual and action-oriented, not alarming
- **Deceased account handling** — Use respectful, empathetic language
- **Complaints and disputes** — Acknowledge the customer's concern, then guide them to resolution
- **Loan defaults or penalties** — Be clear about consequences without being threatening

---

## AI Readability

Because this content will be consumed by AI systems:

- Write **complete, self-contained** sentences (avoid pronouns without clear antecedents)
- Use **descriptive headings** that match how customers phrase questions
- Avoid **ambiguous references** ("this", "that", "the above") — be specific
- Include **key terms** naturally in the text for better semantic retrieval
